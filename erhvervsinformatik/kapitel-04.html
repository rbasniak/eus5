DesignprocesserDesign processes

Måske har du tænkt på, hvordan forskelligeappspå din mobiltelefon, eller den håndscanner, du bruger i dit fritidsjob, er blevet til, og hvordan de netop er blevet til de versioner, du anvender?Perhaps you have thought about how different apps on your mobile phone, or the hand scanner you use in your spare time, came to be and how they just came to be the versions you use?

Here we review two of the most common: the iterative design process and the linear design process.

In this way, the risk of developing an artifact that does not create value in the industry and that no one will buy is reduced.

Lineær designprocesLinear design process

Designprocesser behøver ikke nødvendigvis at foregå iterativt. En anden metode er at styre processen gennem en række bestemte faser.Design processes do not necessarily have to take place iteratively. Another method is to control the process through a series of specific phases.

En lineær fasemodel, ofte kaldt for en vandfaldsmodel, skal forstås som en sekventiel udviklingsmodel, hvor udviklingsprocessen opdeles i afgrænsede faser. Det vil sige, at en fase skal afsluttes, før en ny påbegyndes.A linear phase model, often called a waterfall model, must be understood as a sequential development model, where the development process is divided into defined phases. This means that one phase must be completed before a new one can begin.

Lineære fasemodeller bruges ofte til udvikling af IT-systemer. Modellen kræver, at man kender alle kravene til systemet, inden man starter udviklingsprocessen.Linear phase models are often used for the development of IT systems. The model requires that you know all the requirements for the system before starting the development process.

En fordel ved at kende alle kravene til systemet er, at man kan lave en plan for den samlede udviklingsproces, og dermed også kan beregne en pris og indgå en aftale omkring leverance af IT-systemet. Omvendt er ulempen, at modellen er meget statisk. Når først kravene er definerede, er det dyrt at ændre dem. Desuden er der ikke så megen brugerinvolvering i processen. Man risikerer i værste fald at bruge år på at udvikle et IT-system for derefter at finde ud af, at systemet ikke passer til brugernes behov. Dette er set mange gange i forbindelse med udvikling af størreoffentligeIT-systemer.An advantage of knowing all the requirements for the system is that you can make a plan for the overall development process, and thus also be able to calculate a price and enter into an agreement regarding delivery of the IT system. Conversely, the disadvantage is that the model is very static. Once the requirements are defined, it is expensive to change them. Furthermore, there is not so much user involvement in the process. In the worst case scenario, you risk spending years developing an IT system and then finding out that the system does not suit the users' needs. This has been seen many times in connection with the development of larger public IT systems.

En anerkendt of ofte anvendt model hertil er Double Diamond modellen.In the real world, interaction designers rarely work purely iteratively or linearly when developing digital artefacts. Often works in a kind of mixture of the two methods. A recognized or often used model for this is the Double Diamond model.

Double Diamond er en udviklingsprocesmodel for brugercentreret design, det vil sige at den har meget tilfælles med den iterative metode, men samtidig er den opbygget så den følger en række fast definerede faser, som gør, at den også indeholder mere lineære elementer.Double Diamond is a development process model for user-centred design, i.e. it has a lot in common with the iterative method, but at the same time it is structured so that it follows a series of firmly defined phases, which means that it also contains more linear elements.

Den består af fire faser som hjælper med at sikre, at man både finder det rigtige problem og arbejder frem mod en god løsning. Modellen er opdelt i to diamanter, hvor den første handler om at forstå problemet, og den anden handler om at finde en løsning. Undervejs arbejder man både med at åbne op og få mange idéer og perspektiver frem, og med at snævre ind, så man vælger det vigtigste fokus.It consists of four phases which help to ensure that you both find the right problem and work towards a good solution. The model is divided into two diamonds, where the first is about understanding the problem and the second is about finding a solution. Along the way, you work both to open up and bring out many ideas and perspectives, and to narrow down so that you choose the most important focus.

4.4.1 Brugervenlighed4.4.1 usability

Målet for designprocessen er, at du lykkes med at få designet etartefakt, som løser den forestående problemstilling. Men det er samtidig vigtigt, at du også lykkes med at designe et artefakt, som brugerne ikke frustreres over at bruge. Det skal altså være gennemskueligt, hvilke handlinger der får systemet til at virke, og brugergrænsefladen skal være nem at bruge. Dette kaldes for brugervenlighed.The goal of the design process is for you to succeed in designing an artefact that solves the problem at hand. But at the same time it is important that you also succeed in designing an artefact that users do not get frustrated using. It must therefore be transparent which actions make the system work, and the user interface must be easy to use. This is called usability.

Brugervenlighedusability

Etdigitaltartefakt er brugervenligt, når det er:A digital artefact is user-friendly when it is:

1. Nemt at lære at bruge
Artefaktet skal være så intuitivt at lære at bruge som muligt. Så kommer brugeren hurtigt i gang med at anvende artefaktet, og har samtidigt lettere ved at huske, hvordan artefaktet skal bruges. Der tales om indlæringstid som den tid, brugeren tager om at lære at bruge artefaktet korrekt. Hvis brugeren ikke har benyttet sig af artefaktet i et stykke tid eller kun bruger artefaktet en gang i mellem, tales der om genindlæringstid som den tid, det tager brugeren at lære at bruge artefaktet korrekt igen.1. Easy to learn to use The artifact must be as intuitive to learn to use as possible. Then the user quickly gets started using the artefact, and at the same time has an easier time remembering how the artefact should be used. Learning time is referred to as the time it takes the user to learn how to use the artifact correctly. If the user has not used the artifact for a while or only uses the artifact once in a while, relearning time is referred to as the time it takes the user to learn how to use the artifact correctly again.

2. Effektivt og til at stole på
Ved brug af artefaktet har brugerne en naturlig forventning om at lykkes med en handling, fx at løse en bestemt opgave, søge information, betjene en robot e.l. Dette skal ske på en måde, der er acceptabel for brugeren. Artefaktet skal også være pålideligt at bruge, være tilgængeligt og samtidig rumme data, brugeren kan stole på.2. Effective and reliable When using the artefact, users have a natural expectation of succeeding in an action, e.g. solving a specific task, searching for information, operating a robot, etc. This must be done in a way that is acceptable to the user. The artifact must also be reliable to use, be accessible, and at the same time contain data the user can trust.

3. Sikkert
Brugerne skal være trygge ved at bruge artefaktet, også i forhold til at ønskede handlinger bliver gemt korrekt i artefaktet. Brugeren skal ikke komme til at begå alvorlige fejl ved forkert brug af artefaktet.3. Secure Users must be secure in using the artefact, also in relation to desired actions being stored correctly in the artefact. The user must not make serious mistakes by using the artifact incorrectly.

Jakob Nielsens 10 heuristikker til godt interaktionsdesignJakob Nielsen's 10 heuristics for good interaction design

Den danske forsker i interaktionsdesign, Jakob Nielsen, har udviklet 10 Heuristikker( Ordforklaring: Heuristikker = tommelfingerregler til at vurdere, om et artefakt er brugervenligt), som udviklere kan bruge for at opnå et godt design. Her er fokus på den feedback et artefakt giver, når brugeren anvender det.The Danish researcher in interaction design, Jakob Nielsen, has developed 10 Heuristics (Explanation: Heuristics = rules of thumb to assess whether an artifact is user-friendly), which developers can use to achieve a good design. Here the focus is on the feedback an artifact gives when the user uses it.

1. Synlighed af systemstatus1. Visibility of system status

Artefaktet skal vise hvad der sker: Løsningen skal hele tiden holde brugeren informeret gennem tydelig feedback, fx "gemt" eller "loader". Det skal ske inden for rimelig tid. På den måde giver der overblik og tryghed til brugeren.The artifact must show what is happening: The solution must constantly keep the user informed through clear feedback, for example "saved" or "loader". This must be done within a reasonable time. In this way, it gives the user an overview and security.

2. Match med virkeligheden2. Match with reality

Artefaktet skal tale brugerens sprog: Brug almindelige ord, symboler og begreber, som brugeren kender – ikke teknisk sprog, samt sørg for at information vises i en naturlig og logisk rækkefølge.The artifact must speak the user's language: Use common words, symbols and concepts that the user knows - not technical language, and ensure that information is displayed in a natural and logical order.

3. Brugerens kontrol og frihed3. User control and freedom

Det skal være muligt at fortryde i artefaktet: Brugeren skal kunne komme ud af "fejl-handlinger", fx "tilbage", "annuller", "fortryd". Dermed gøres det trygt at bruge artefaktet.It must be possible to undo in the artefact: the user must be able to get out of "error actions", eg "back", "cancel", "undo". This makes it safe to use the artifact.

4. Konsistens og standarder4. Consistency and standards

Artefaktet skal være ens og genkendeligt fra lignende løsninger: Sammefunktionerog begreber skal betyde det samme overalt.The artifact must be similar and recognizable from similar solutions: Same functions and terms must mean the same everywhere.

Følg kendte standarder fx hvordan menuer og knapper fungerer. Derved undgår man, at bruge skal bruge tid på at regne ud, hvordan artefaktet skal bruges.Follow known standards, e.g. how menus and buttons work. This avoids the user having to spend time figuring out how the artifact is to be used.

5. Forebyggelse af fejl5. Prevention of errors

Artefaktet skal forhindre fejl: Det er bedre at undgå fejl end at rette dem bagefter. Det kan gøres ved at systemet tjekker input, inden det går videre, eller beder brugeren bekræfte input/handlingen. Dermed opnår en tryghed i at bruge artefaktet.The artifact must prevent errors: It is better to avoid errors than to correct them afterwards. This can be done by the system checking the input before proceeding, or by asking the user to confirm the input/action. This gives you confidence in using the artifact.

6. Genkendelse frem for hukommelse6. Recognition rather than memory

Artefaktet skal være nemt at aflæse. Brugeren skal ikke gå og huske på, hvordan elementer fungerer – de skal hurtigt kunne tyde mulighederne, Fx menuer, ikoner og synlige instruktioner.The artifact must be easy to read. The user does not have to remember how elements work - they must be able to quickly decipher the options, eg menus, icons and visible instructions.

7. Fleksibilitet og effektivitet7. Flexibility and efficiency

Artefaktet skal passe til alle brugere - både nybegyndere og erfarne. Fx kan man oprette muligheden for genveje til ekspertbrugere og samtidig tydelige simple løsninger til nye brugere.The artifact must be suitable for all users - both beginners and experienced. For example, you can create the possibility of shortcuts for expert users and at the same time clear simple solutions for new users.

8. Æstetisk og minimalistisk design8. Aesthetic and minimalist design

Artefaktet skal være enkelt, hvor kun relevante informationer skal vises. For meget indhold gør det sværere at bruge.The artifact must be simple, where only relevant information must be displayed. Too much content makes it harder to use.

9. Hjælp ved fejlhåndtering9. Help with error handling

Artefaktet skal forklare fejl klart og tydeligt. Fejlmeddelelser skal være i almindeligt sprog og forklare problemet + løsning.The artifact must explain errors clearly and distinctly. Error messages must be in plain language and explain the problem + solution.

Ikke tekniske fejlkoder. Fx hvis felter mangler at udfyldt for at systemet kan gå videre.Not technical error codes. For example, if fields need to be filled in for the system to proceed.

10. Hjælp og dokumentation10. Help and documentation

Artefaktet skal kunne hjælpe brugeren. Der skal være nem adgang til hjælp fx FAQ eller guide.The artifact must be able to help the user. There must be easy access to help, e.g. FAQ or guide.

Hjælpen skal være konkret og knyttet til opgaven.The help must be concrete and linked to the task.

I løbet af designprocessen er det vigtigt, at du løbende får afprøvet og vurderet dine prototyper.During the design process, it is important that you continuously test and evaluate your prototypes.

The tasks and most of the questions are planned in advance in an interview guide.

Det er vigtigt at forberede dig grundigt til gennemførelsen af tænke-højt-testen, for du skal være sikker på, at du får svar på de rigtige ting. Therefore, it is also a test that takes a relatively long time to perform. Conversely, the test provides extensive feedback on the users' use of the digital artefact, which contributes important knowledge in relation to the user-friendliness of the prototype of the digital artefact as well as any errors and shortcomings in the prototype.

Under en tænke-højt-test er der fokus på brugernes adfærd ved brugen af prototypen af det digitale artefakt. Therefore, it is the role of the interviewer to get the user to express his actions and thoughts during the test.   • Indsæt selv flere eksempler.Insert more examples yourself.
