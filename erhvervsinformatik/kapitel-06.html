6.1 Virksomhedens IT-system6.1 The company's IT system

Enterprise Resource PlanningEnterprise Resource Planning

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) betyder planlægning af virksomhedensressourcer.Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) means planning of the company's resources.

Virksomheden råder over mange ressourcer. Jo bedre virksomheden er til at planlægge og styre sine ressourcer, jo bedre står den i konkurrencen. Derfor har de fleste virksomheder et ERP-system, der samler alle virksomhedens arbejdsområder ogarbejdsprocesseri ét fælles IT-system.The company has many resources at its disposal. The better the company is at planning and managing its resources, the better it stands in the competition. Therefore, most companies have an ERP system that brings together all the company's work areas and work processes in one common IT system.

Dine arbejdsopgaver i virksomheden afgør, hvilke af virksomhedens ressourcer du skal styre.Your tasks in the company determine which of the company's resources you must manage.

  • Indkøbsafdelingen styrer indkøbene.The purchasing department manages the purchases.
  • Lagerafdelingen styrer logistikken.The warehouse department manages the logistics.
  • Produktionsafdelingen styrer produktionen.The production department manages the production.
  • Salgsafdelingen styrer relationerne til kunderne.The sales department manages relations with customers.
  • Økonomiafdelingen styrer økonomien.The finance department manages the finances.
  • HR-afdelingenstyrer de menneskelige ressourcer osv.The HR department manages human resources, etc.

ERP-systemerERP systems

Der findes mange forskelligeERP-systemerfx e-conomic, Navision, Axapta, C5 og SAP, fordi virksomhederne har forskellige behov. De fleste ERP-systemer minder dog meget om hinanden, så når først du har lært at arbejde i ét ERP-system, vil du ofte kunne bruge din viden til at arbejde i et andet ERP-system.There are many different ERP systems, e.g. e-conomic, Navision, Axapta, C5 and SAP, because companies have different needs. However, most ERP systems are very similar to each other, so once you have learned to work in one ERP system, you will often be able to use your knowledge to work in another ERP system.

Kernen iERP-systemeter en fællesdatabase, hvor alle informationer for hele virksomheden opbevares. Teknisk set er databasen bygget op af forskellige moduler, som virksomheden selv kan sammensætte og tilpasse efter individuelt behov og branche. De mest almindelige moduler i et ERP-system er:The core of the ERP system is a common database where all information for the entire company is stored. Technically, the database is made up of various modules, which the company itself can put together and adapt according to individual needs and industry. The most common modules in an ERP system are:

  • Supply Chain Management (SCM) til styring af indkøb og logistik.Supply Chain Management (SCM) for managing purchases and logistics.
  • Manufacturing Resource Planning (MRP) til styring af produktion - gælder kun produktionsvirksomheder.Manufacturing Resource Planning (MRP) for managing production - only applies to manufacturing companies.
  • Customer Relationship Management (CRM) til håndtering af kunderelationer.Customer Relationship Management (CRM) for managing customer relationships.
  • Finance Resource Management (FRM) til styring af virksomhedens økonomi.Finance Resource Management (FRM) for managing the company's finances.
  • Human Resource Management (HRM) til håndtering af de menneskelige ressourcer.Human Resource Management (HRM) for managing human resources.

TillægsmodulerAdditional modules

I videoen kan du få indsigt i, hvilke muligheder virksomhederne har for at købe tillægsmoduler hos e-conomic.In the video, you can gain insight into what options companies have to purchase additional modules from e-conomic.

ERP-systemet skaber et "real-time" billede af virksomhedens ressourcer, da dataene opdateres, når en medarbejder foretager en registrering i systemet. Det betyder fx, at lige så snart lagermedarbejderen varemodtager enordrei systemet, så kan salgsafdelingen se, at varerne er på lager. Det er ret smart, for så kan salgsafdelingen med det samme give kunden besked om, at varerne er på lager og kan afsendes, når kunden ønsker det. Derudover kan salgsmedarbejderen i CRM-modulet se:The ERP system creates a "real-time" picture of the company's resources, as the data is updated when an employee registers in the system. This means, for example, that as soon as the warehouse worker receives an order in the system, the sales department can see that the goods are in stock. This is quite smart, because then the sales department can immediately notify the customer that the goods are in stock and can be shipped when the customer wants it. In addition, the sales employee can see in the CRM module:

  • Om den pågældende kunde skal have rabat.Whether the customer in question should receive a discount.
  • Hvor lang kredit kunden må tilbydes.How long credit the customer may be offered.

Generelt hjælper ERP-systemet altså med at effektivisere opsamling og brug af data i virksomheden.In general, the ERP system therefore helps to streamline the collection and use of data in the company.

Eksempel: Arbejdsprocesser i et ERP-systemExample: Work processes in an ERP system

Ovenfor har vi skitseret eksempler på arbejdsprocesser i en virksomhed og arbejdsprocessernes påvirkning i ERP-systemets forskellige moduler:Above we have outlined examples of work processes in a company and the impact of the work processes in the different modules of the ERP system:

  1. Indkøbsafdelingen har indkøbt varer, som lagerpersonalet har varemodtaget i ERP-systemet (SCM). Salgsafdelingen kan nu informere kunderne om varernes lagerstatus (CRM).The purchasing department has purchased goods that the warehouse staff has received in the ERP system (SCM). The sales department can now inform customers about the stock status of the goods (CRM).
  2. Indkøbsafdelingen registrerer varekøbet i ERP-systemet (SCM), så økonomiafdelingen ved, at de skal betale fakturaen (FRM).The purchasing department registers the goods purchase in the ERP system (SCM), so that the finance department knows that they have to pay the invoice (FRM).
  3. Salgsafdelingen sælger varer og registrerer dette i ERP-systemet (CRM). Indkøbsafdelingen kan herefter se, at der snart skal bestilles nye varer hjem (SCM).The sales department sells goods and registers this in the ERP system (CRM). The purchasing department can then see that new goods will soon have to be ordered home (SCM).
  4. Salgsafdelingen registrerer salget i ERP-systemet (CRM), så økonomiafdelingen ved, at de skal sende kunden enfaktura(FRM).The sales department registers the sale in the ERP system (CRM), so the finance department knows to send the customer an invoice (FRM).
  5. HR-afdelingen opretter medarbejdere i ERP-systemet (HRM), så økonomiafdelingen ved, at der skal udbetales løn til de ansatte (FRM).The HR department creates employees in the ERP system (HRM), so that the finance department knows that wages must be paid to the employees (FRM).

Fordele og ulemper ved ERP-systemerAdvantages and disadvantages of ERP systems

Ved at samle alle moduler i ét ERP-system slipper virksomhedens ledere og medarbejdere for at skulle arbejde i flere forskellige systemer på samme tid. Hvis de forskellige systemer ikke snakker sammen, er medarbejderne også nødt til at registrere informationer flere gange. Så det kan være en fordel at samle alle virksomhedens informationer i ét ERP-system.By bringing together all modules in one ERP system, the company's managers and employees are freed from having to work in several different systems at the same time. If the different systems do not talk to each other, the employees also have to register information several times. So it can be advantageous to collect all the company's information in one ERP system.

Omvendt er det sårbart at samle alle informationer i ét ERP-system. Forestil dig, at systemet var nede, så ingen kunne arbejde, eller at ERP-systemet blev hacket, så alle informationerne var væk. Hvilken katastrofe!Conversely, it is vulnerable to collect all information in one ERP system. Imagine that the system was down so that no one could work, or that the ERP system was hacked so that all the information was gone. What a disaster!

Kommunikation mellem ERP-systemerCommunication between ERP systems

Udover at kommunikere med andre virksomheder via e-mail har virksomheden også mulighed for at sende dokumenter elektronisk viaEDI. For at virksomheden kan udveksle data elektronisk med sin samarbejdspartner, må deres computere tale samme sprog eller have en fælles standard, som man kalder det. Den mest udbredte standard i Danmark er EDIFACT (se forklaring nedenfor).In addition to communicating with other companies via e-mail, the company also has the option of sending documents electronically via EDI. In order for the company to exchange data electronically with its business partner, their computers must speak the same language or have a common standard, as you call it. The most widespread standard in Denmark is EDIFACT (see explanation below).

EDIEDI

EDI er en forkortelse afElectronic Data Interchange, der betyder udveksling af elektroniske data.EDI is an abbreviation of Electronic Data Interchange, which means exchange of electronic data.

Eksempel: Data i dokumenterExample: Data in documents

I en virksomhed er der mange forskellige slags dokumenter fx indkøbsordrer, fakturaer, salgsrapporter, testresultater, leveringsinformationer m.fl.In a company, there are many different kinds of documents, e.g. purchase orders, invoices, sales reports, test results, delivery information, etc.

Dokumenterne ser selvfølgelig meget forskellige ud. En EDI-standard bestemmer, hvor i dokumentet bestemte oplysninger kan findes. Det kan være oplysninger om fx virksomhedens navn, varenummer, antal eller varens pris. Disse oplysninger laves om til data, der kan sendes videre til andre IT-systemer.Of course, the documents look very different. An EDI standard determines where in the document certain information can be found. It can be information about, for example, the company's name, item number, quantity or the price of the item. This information is converted into data that can be forwarded to other IT systems.

EDIFACTEDIFACT

EDIFACT er en forkortelse af Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce and Transport.EDIFACT is short for Electronic Data Interchange for Administration, Commerce and Transport.

EDIFACT er den mest anvendte internationale EDI-standard, især i Europa og Asien. Den kan bruges til mange forskellige slags dokumenter. Der findes også standarder, der er lavet mere specifikt til en bestemt branche fx detailhandlen (EANCOM) eller den tyske bilindustri (VDA).EDIFACT is the most widely used international EDI standard, especially in Europe and Asia. It can be used for many different types of documents. There are also standards that have been made more specifically for a specific industry, e.g. the retail trade (EANCOM) or the German car industry (VDA).

Det er dog ikke nok at have en fælles standard. Virksomheden må også installere en specielsoftware, hvis den vil benytte sig af EDI. EDI-softwaren gør det muligt for virksomheder at kommunikere elektronisk uafhængigt af IT-udstyr.However, it is not enough to have a common standard. The company must also install special software if it wants to use EDI. The EDI software enables companies to communicate electronically independently of IT equipment.

Nedenfor ser du et eksempel på elektronisk udveksling af data via EDI.Below you see an example of electronic exchange of data via EDI.

Figuren viser, at en kinesisk leverandør skal sende en faktura fra sit ERP-system til sin danske kundes ERP-system.The figure shows that a Chinese supplier must send an invoice from its ERP system to its Danish customer's ERP system.

Da vi ikke kan læse kinesiske skrifttegn, skal informationerne i dokumentet findes og kodes om, så vi forstår dem:Since we cannot read Chinese characters, the information in the document must be found and recoded so that we understand it:

  • Ved hjælp af EDI-softwaren laves fakturaen på kinesisk om til EDI-sproget EDIFACT.Using the EDI software, the Chinese invoice is converted into the EDI language EDIFACT.
  • EDI-dataene sendes viainternettettil den danske kunde.The EDI data is sent via the Internet to the Danish customer.
  • For at kunden kan læse fakturaen, kodes EDI-dataene om til dansk ved hjælp af EDI-softwaren.In order for the customer to be able to read the invoice, the EDI data is recoded into Danish using the EDI software.

Når data sendes elektronisk via EDI, har det altså ikke nogen betydning, hvilkenhardwareeller software samarbejdspartneren benytter. Det kræver blot, at begge virksomheder har EDI-softwaren.When data is sent electronically via EDI, it does not matter which hardware or software the business partner uses. It simply requires that both companies have the EDI software.

Det svarer til, at jeg kan være ligeglad med, om du arbejder på en Mac med OS X somstyresystem, selv om jeg arbejder på en pc med Windows som styresystem. For virksomhedernes IT-systemer behøver ikke at være kompatible, når de har investeret i EDI-softwaren.This is equivalent to me not caring if you work on a Mac with OS X as an operating system, even if I work on a PC with Windows as an operating system. Because the companies' IT systems do not need to be compatible when they have invested in the EDI software.

EDI-softwaren arbejder sammen med virksomhedens IT-systemer bl.a. deres ERP-system og servere. Med EDI er det nemt at udveksle produktionstal, lageroplysninger osv. Det er også muligt at lade EDI-softwaren hente data fra samarbejdspartneren, uden at nogen i den modsatte ende skal trykke på en send-knap.The EDI software works together with the company's IT systems, e.g. their ERP system and servers. With EDI, it is easy to exchange production figures, stock information, etc. It is also possible to let the EDI software retrieve data from the business partner without anyone on the other end having to press a send button.

Når virksomheden og dens samarbejdspartnere udveksler dokumenter elektronisk, kan begge parter opnå en stor besparelse. Leverandøren behøver fx ikke at bruge tid på at taste kundensordreind, da dataene sendes direkte fra kundensERP-systemtil leverandørensERP-system. Samtidig kan leverandøren undgå irriterende tastefejl, som altid sniger sig ind, når vi taster informationermanuelt.When the company and its business partners exchange documents electronically, both parties can achieve a large saving. For example, the supplier does not need to spend time entering the customer's order, as the data is sent directly from the customer's ERP system to the supplier's ERP system. At the same time, the supplier can avoid annoying typos, which always creep in when we enter information manually.

Udover at virksomheden undgår irriterende tastefejl, er der følgende fordele for virksomheden ved at indføre EDI:In addition to the company avoiding annoying typos, there are the following advantages for the company by introducing EDI:

  • Mindre arbejde med genindtastning.Less work with re-entry.
  • Mindre kontrol ved genindtastning.Less checking when re-entering.
  • Mindre risiko for fejl ved genindtastning.Less risk of re-entry errors.
  • Hurtigere og bedre kommunikation.Faster and better communication.
  • Tættere og bedre samarbejde.Closer and better cooperation.

APIAPI

APIer en forkortelse afApplication Programming Interface. API er et værktøj, som forbinder software eller IT-systemer, så de kan udveksle data.API is short for Application Programming Interface. API is a tool that connects software or IT systems so that they can exchange data.

Ligesom udveksling af data ved hjælp af EDI kræver en speciel EDI-software, kræver udveksling af data ved hjælp af API også en special software, nemlig en applikation (enapp).Just as the exchange of data using EDI requires special EDI software, the exchange of data using API also requires special software, namely an application (an app ).

MobilePay anvender API til bl.a. at tilbyde mobilbetaling, så du kan betale med din MobilePay app, både når du handler i fysiske butikker og på internettet.MobilePay uses the API for e.g. to offer mobile payment, so you can pay with your MobilePay app, both when you shop in physical stores and on the internet.

I virksomheden Visma e-conomic anvendes API til at tilbyde integration mellem fx en webshop og regnskabssystemet, så data mellem de to dele udveksles automatisk.In the company Visma e-conomic, API is used to offer integration between, for example, a webshop and the accounting system, so that data between the two parts is exchanged automatically.

API gør det lettere for virksomheder at udveksle data både internt og eksternt, og virksomheden kan derfor spare mange arbejdstimer og ressourcer.API makes it easier for companies to exchange data both internally and externally, and the company can therefore save many working hours and resources.

Virksomheden skal selvfølgelig være opmærksom på brugernes sikkerhed, især hvis virksomheden udveksler persondata.Of course, the company must pay attention to the safety of users, especially if the company exchanges personal data.

6.2 3-lags arkitektur (C)6.2 3-layer architecture (C)

Forespørgsler og svarQuestions and answers

Lad os sige, at vi vil åbne websiden:www.eksempel.dk. Vi indtaster adressen ibrowseren, der så kontakter den websideserver, som svarer til adressen. Serveren er en maskine, der er tilkobletinternettet, og som altid er tændt og klar til at modtage forespørgsler frabrowsere. Når browseren forespørger indholdet af www.eksempel.dk, sørger serveren for at udlevere indholdet. Denne enkle kommunikation i to trin består af:Let's say we want to open the website: www .eksempel.dk. We enter the address in the browser, which then contacts the web page server that corresponds to the address. The server is a machine that is connected to the Internet and is always on and ready to receive requests from browsers. When the browser queries the content of www.eksempel.dk, the server provides the content. This simple two-step communication consists of:

  • Enforespørgsel(request)A request
  • Et svar (response)A response (response)

Generelt kommunikerer programmer over nettet ved at udveksle beskeder. Forespørgsler og svar er eksempler på beskeder.In general, programs communicate over the network by exchanging messages. Requests and responses are examples of messages.

Klient-server-princippetThe client-server principle

Request-response-forløbet i en kommunikation mellem browser og server er et eksempel på et designmønster, nemlig klient-server-princippet.The request-response process in a communication between browser and server is an example of a design pattern, namely the client-server principle.

Klienten forespørger tjenester hos serveren, og serveren svarer ved at levere de forespurgte services:The client requests services from the server, and the server responds by providing the requested services:

  • Klienten er aktiv – tager initiativ til en forespørgsel.The client is active – initiates an inquiry.
  • Serveren er passiv – svarer bare på forespørgsler.The server is passive – just responds to requests.

I virksomheden erklientenden computer, medarbejderen bruger til sit arbejde fx til at skrive en mail til leverandøren. Mailprogrammet ligger på virksomhedens server, derfor sender medarbejderen via klienten en forespørgsel til serveren.In the company, the client is the computer the employee uses for his work, for example to write an email to the supplier. The mail program is on the company's server, so the employee sends a request to the server via the client.

Klienten er normalt i kontakt med en bruger. I klient-server-situationer vil vi derfor ofte referere til "brugeren", underforstået at der sidder en bruger fx en medarbejder og styrer klientens forespørgsler.The client is usually in contact with a user. In client-server situations, we will therefore often refer to the "user", with the understanding that there is a user, for example an employee, who controls the client's requests.

Serverener typisk en væsentligt kraftigere maskine, og en virksomhed kan godt have flere servere til hver sin opgave fx en mailserver, en webserver osv. Dette er ofte tilfældet i store virksomheder, fordi der vil være mange forespørgsler fra klienterne på én gang.The server is typically a significantly more powerful machine, and a company may well have several servers for each task, e.g. a mail server, a web server, etc. This is often the case in large companies, because there will be many requests from the clients at once.

Servere betjenes sjældent direkte af mennesker, derfor er der som regel hverken skærm, tastatur eller mus tilkoblet.Servers are rarely operated directly by people, therefore there is usually neither a screen, keyboard or mouse connected.

Eksempel: Typer af servereExample: Types of servers

  • Webserverentilbyder adgang til webindhold. Dens klienter er typisk browsere.The web server offers access to web content. Its clients are typically browsers.
  • Spilserverentilbyder adgang til at spille et onlinespil. Dens klienter er spilprogrammer.The game server offers access to play an online game. Its clients are game programs.
  • Printserverentilbyder adgang til en eller flere printere. Dens klienter er tit tekstbehandlingsprogrammer, hvor brugeren beder om at få printet dokumenter.The print server offers access to one or more printers. Its clients are often word processing programs where the user asks to print documents.
  • Filserverentilbyder adgang til en samling filer. Dens klienter er særlige programmer, der kan hente filerne til brugerens computer.The file server offers access to a collection of files. Its clients are special programs that can download the files to the user's computer.

3-lags arkitektur i software3-lags arkitektur i software

3-lags arkitekturen er en videreudvikling af klient-server-princippet og består af tre lag:The 3-layer architecture is a further development of the client-server principle and consists of three layers:

  1. PræsentationslagetThe presentation layer
  2. LogiklagetThe logic layer
  3. DatalagetThe data layer

Præsentationslaget svarer til klient-delen i klient-server-princippet.The presentation layer corresponds to the client part of the client-server principle.

Logiklaget og datalaget er en videreopdeling af server-delen i klient-server-princippet.The logic layer and the data layer are a further division of the server part of the client-server principle.

3-lags arkitektur isoftwarehandler om, at programmer kan inddeles i tre lag:3-layer architecture in software is about the fact that programs can be divided into three layers:

1. Præsentationslaget1. The presentation layer

  • Input fra brugeren ved hjælp af tastatur, museklik, berøring o.l.Input from the user using keyboard, mouse click, touch etc.
  • Præsentation af grafik, tekst, billeder osv. til brugeren.Presentation of graphics, text, images, etc. to the user.

2. Logiklaget2. Logiklaget

  • Håndterer dataudveksling mellem præsentationslaget og datalaget fx logiske beregninger og andre manipulationer på data fra datalaget.Handles data exchange between the presentation layer and the data layer, e.g. logical calculations and other manipulations on data from the data layer.

3. Datalaget3. The data layer

  • Opbevarer data.Stores data.
  • Søger i data.Searches in data.
  • Sender data til logiklaget.Sends data to the logic layer.

Fordele og ulemper ved hhv. klient-server og tre-lags-arkitekturAdvantages and disadvantages of respectively client-server and three-tier architecture

Eksempel: WebshopExample: Webshop

Forestil dig, at du besøger en webshop, hvor du vil søge efter et bestemt produkt fx en jakke.Imagine that you visit a webshop where you want to search for a specific product, for example a jacket.

Præsentationslageter det, du som kunde kan se og interagere med via din browser, altså selve brugergrænsefladen for webshoppen.The presentation layer is what you as a customer can see and interact with via your browser, i.e. the user interface for the webshop itself.

Du benytter dig afsøgefeltetpå brugergrænsefladen og indtaster inputtet "jakke". Denne information modtages nu aflogiklaget,som kontakterdatalaget.Datalaget sender nu samtlige registrerede jakker retur til logiklaget, som igen kontakter præsentationslaget, hvor jakkerne bliver vist på brugergrænsefladen.You use the search field on the user interface and enter the input "jacket". This information is now received by the logic layer, which contacts the data layer. The data layer now sends all registered jackets back to the logic layer, which in turn contacts the presentation layer, where the jackets are displayed on the user interface.

Internettet vs. World Wide WebThe Internet vs. World Wide Web

Et af menneskehedens største bedrifter er nok opfindelsen af internettet. For dig er internettet og World Wide Web (WWW) måske det samme. Du tænker nok ikke over, at der er forskel. To you, the Internet and the World Wide Web (WWW) may be the same thing. You probably don't think that there is a difference. But it is important to distinguish between the Internet and the WWW, as different technologies are behind them.

Det fysiske internet kaldes på engelsk "the net" eller "the internet", mens "the web" (nettet) ofte bruges om World Wide Web. I Danmark bruger vi ordet "internet" både om det fysiske internet og World Wide Web. InternettetThe Internet

Internettet eret verdensomspændende fysisk netværk, der består af computernetværk, altså et netværk af computernetværk.The Internet is a worldwide physical network consisting of computer networks, i.e. a network of computer networks.

Internettet består af kabelforbindelser og vigtige knudepunkter, såkaldtecoreroutere. Disse routere er særligt velegnede til at sende data hurtigt videre i netværket. Knudepunkterne forbindes med fiberoptiske kabler, der kan sende data videre med lysets hastighed. Men ældre telefonlinjer og satellitforbindelser er også en vigtig del af det fysiske netværk.The Internet consists of cable connections and important nodes, so-called core routers. These routers are particularly suitable for sending data quickly through the network. The nodes are connected with fiber optic cables that can transmit data at the speed of light. But legacy phone lines and satellite connections are also an important part of the physical network.

Det fysiske netværk ejes af de lande, hvor kablerne og hardwaren befinder sig, samt af de store virksomheder fxTDCellerAT&T, der arbejder med kommunikation. Netværketshardwareog kabler skal jævnligt opdateres for at følge udviklingen.The physical network is owned by the countries where the cables and hardware are located, as well as by the large companies such as TDC or AT&T that work with communications. The network's hardware and cables must be regularly updated to keep up with developments.

En host er en server eller computer, der er fysisk koblet til internettets netværk.The Internet works in such a way that all over the globe there are web servers and hosts switched on around the clock. A host is a server or computer that is physically connected to the Internet's network.

Derfra bliver du via deres fysiske forbindelse sendt ud på internettet.When you are on the Internet, you connect via your modem, broadband, mobile network or Wi-Fi. In fact, you first get a connection to the server of your internet provider. From there, you will be sent out to the internet via their physical connection.

Derefter har du mulighed for at surfe rundt og tjekke indhold og tjenester fra de forskellige webservere og hosts, som står til rådighed og er forbundet via World Wide Web.You then have the opportunity to surf around and check out content and services from the various web servers and hosts that are available and connected via the World Wide Web.

World Wide WebWorld Wide Web

World Wide Web blev skabt i 1989 af Sir Tim Berners-Lee. Han fik idéen til at brugehyperlinksmellem dokumenter. Disse links gør, at man kan forbinde ord og dokumenter på kryds og tværs af hjemmesider.The World Wide Web was created in 1989 by Sir Tim Berners-Lee. He came up with the idea of ​​using hyperlinks between documents. These links make it possible to connect words and documents across websites.

World Wide Web er en række standarder eller aftaler, som bruges på hjemmesider til overførsel af filerne på det fysiske netværk, altså internettet. De data, som en hjemmeside består af, skal overholde standarderne for World Wide Web, såbrowserenved, hvad den skal vise dig.The World Wide Web is a series of standards or agreements that are used on websites to transfer the files on the physical network, i.e. the Internet. The data that a website consists of must comply with the standards of the World Wide Web so that the browser knows what to show you.

Når du går kobler dig på internettet for at se en hjemmeside eller denne iBog, bruger du en browser til at hente informationerne. Du bruger måskeMicrosoft Edge,SafariellerGoogle Chrome. En browser henter websider fra en eller flere webservere og viser dem på din skærm. Browseren kan læse og navigere i store datamængder, især hypertekst.When you connect to the Internet to view a website or this iBook, you use a browser to retrieve the information. You may be using Microsoft Edge, Safari or Google Chrome. A browser retrieves web pages from one or more web servers and displays them on your screen. The browser can read and navigate large amounts of data, especially hypertext.

På den måde kan du få adgang til alle de informationer, som ligger på de forskellige computere eller servere på internettet. Så man kan sige, at World Wide Web "kører" i kablerne på det fysiske netværk.In this way, you can access all the information that is on the various computers or servers on the Internet. So you can say that the World Wide Web "runs" in the cables of the physical network.
