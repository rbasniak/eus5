JYSK B2BJYSK B2B

JYSK B2B sælger til virksomheder og offentlige institutioner i mange størrelser.JYSK B2B sells to companies and public institutions of many sizes.

Kunderne omfatter blandt andet hoteller, restauranter, kontorer, skoler, institutioner og virksomheder, der skal indrette lokaler eller flere værelser.The customers include hotels, restaurants, offices, schools, institutions and companies that need to furnish premises or several rooms.

Se mere påJYSK B2B-webistet.See more on the JYSK B2B website.

KundeværdiCustomer value

JYSK B2B tilbyder erhvervskunder 8 % rabat, også på nedsatte varer, professionel rådgivning, fleksibel levering.JYSK B2B offers business customers an 8% discount, also on discounted items, professional advice, flexible delivery.

Der er mulighed for at handle online og i mere end 100 danske butikker.It is possible to shop online and in more than 100 Danish stores.

Større erhvervskunder kan indgå aftaler om blandt andetsortiment, mængde, pris og levering. Kravene varierer efter kundens branche, indkøbsbehov og anvendelse.Larger business customers can enter into agreements on, among other things, assortment , quantity, price and delivery. The requirements vary according to the customer's industry, purchasing needs and application.

Indkøb og sortimentPurchasing and assortment

B2B-området anvender JYSKs eksisterende sortiment, leverandører og logistik.The B2B area uses JYSK's existing assortment, suppliers and logistics.

Det giver adgang til et bredt sortiment og høj varetilgængelighed. Specialordrer håndteres inden for JYSKs kerneområder.It gives access to a wide assortment and high product availability. Special orders are handled within JYSK's core areas.
