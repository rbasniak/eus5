Boozt.comBoozt.com

Boozt.com er et nordisk onlinevarehus med fem hovedkategorier: Fashion, Kids, Sport, Beauty og Home.Boozt.com is a Nordic online department store with five main categories: Fashion, Kids, Sport, Beauty and Home.

Sortimentet består af omkring 1.600 skandinaviske og internationale mærker i mellem- og premiumsegmentet.the assortment consists of around 1,600 Scandinavian and international brands in the medium and premium segment.

Baggrund og konceptBackground and concept

Boozt blev grundlagt i 2011 og har udviklet sig fra leverandør af e-handelsløsninger til et nordisk onlinevarehus.Boozt was founded in 2011 and has developed from a supplier of e-commerce solutions to a Nordic online warehouse.

Koncernen driver Boozt.com og outletplatformen Booztlet.com og har hovedkontor i Malmø.The group operates Boozt.com and the outlet platform Booztlet.com and is headquartered in Malmö.

Positionering og sortimentPositioning and assortment

Boozt.com positioneres som et onlinevarehus med et udvalg i mellem- og premiumsegmentet.Boozt.com is positioned as an online department store with a selection in the medium and premium segment.

Booztlet.com supplerer med outletvarer og lavere priser.Booztlet.com supplements with outlet items and lower prices.

De to platforme gør det muligt at nå forskellige pris- og livsstilsorienterede målgrupper.The two platforms make it possible to reach different price and lifestyle oriented target groupr.

KonkurrenceCompetition

Booztkonkurrerer med blandt andre Zalando, Amazon og andre internationale platforme.Boozt competes with, among others, Zalando, Amazon and other international platforms.

Virksomheden søger at adskille sig gennem nordisk markedskendskab, et udvalgtsortiment, hurtig levering, kundeservice og egen teknologi.The company seeks to distinguish itself through Nordic market knowledge, a selected assortment , fast delivery, customer service and own technology.

Målgruppertarget groupr

Boozt.com henvender sig især til kunder, der ønsker kvalitet, kendte mærker og en enkel onlineoplevelse.Boozt.com is particularly aimed at customers who want quality, well-known brands and a simple online experience.

Booztlet.com retter sig mod mere prisbevidste kunder.Booztlet.com targets more price-conscious customers.

Club Boozt styrker relationen til loyale kunder gennem personlige tilbud, tidlig adgang til udsalg og anbefalinger.Club Boozt strengthens the relationship with loyal customers through personal offers, early access to sales and recommendations.

Teknologi og logistikTechnology and logistics

Boozts egenudviklede teknologi udgør central dele af forretningsmodellen.Boozt's proprietary technology forms central parts of the business model.

Teknologien bruges blandt andet til kundeoplevelse, markedsføring, lagerstyring og distribution.The technology is used, among other things, for customer experience, marketing, inventory management and distribution.

I 2025 integrerede virksomheden AI bredere i værdikæden.By 2025, the company integrated AI more broadly into the value chain.

BæredygtighedSustainability

Boozts Care-For-strategi skal gøre ansvarlige valg lettere for kunder og partnere.Boozt's Care-For strategy aims to make responsible choices easier for customers and partners.

I 2025 øgede virksomheden andelen af genanvendt affald til 83,3 %, mens 54 % af leverandørerne var tilsluttet bæredygtighedsprogrammer.By 2025, the company increased the proportion of recycled waste to 83.3%, while 54% of suppliers were connected to sustainability programs.

Ledelse og medarbejdereManagement and employees

Boozts kultur bygger på tillid, frihed og ansvar.Boozt's culture is based on trust, freedom and responsibility.

Virksomheden lægger vægt på samarbejde, teknologiske kompetencer og medarbejdernes ansvar for at udvikle kundeoplevelsen og forretningen.The company emphasizes collaboration, technological competences and the employees' responsibility to develop the customer experience and the business.

Hør medarbejdere fortælle om, hvordan det er at arbejde hos Boozt.com.Hear employees talk about what it's like to work at Boozt.com.
