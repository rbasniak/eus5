10.1 Distributionskæden10.1 The distribution chain

Begrebet distribution handler om, hvordan man flytter en vare fra en afsender til en modtager. Når du skal finde ud af, hvordan man flytter varer fra producenten til forbrugeren, vil du opdage, at varen meget ofte kommer igennem flere led på sin vej. Det er derfor denne vej omtales distributionskæden.The concept of distribution is about how to move an item from a sender to a recipient. When you have to figure out how to move goods from the producer to the consumer, you will find that the goods very often go through several stages on their way. This is why the distribution chain is referred to this way.

Grundmodellen, den generelle distributionskæde, kan du se i figur 10.2.The basic model, the general distribution chain, can be seen in figure 10.2.

En vare produceres altid, for at den på et senere tidspunkt skal forbruges. I den generelle distributionskæde kan du se, at varen kan komme igennem yderligere to led: Engroshandelsvirksomhed og detailhandelsvirksomhed. De led kalder vi for mellemhandlere.A product is always produced in order to be consumed at a later time. In the general distribution chain, you can see that the item can go through two more links: Wholesaletrading company and retailtrading company. We call those links intermediaries.

MellemhandlereIntermediaries

Da det kan være en stor opgave for enproduktionsvirksomhedat tage sig af salget af varer til forbrugerne, kan produktionsvirksomheden vælge at sælge sine varer videre til andre virksomheder, der kan tage sig af den opgave, fx en engroshandelsvirksomhed eller en detailhandelsvirksomhed. I kapitel 1 blev engroshandelsvirksomheden kaldt engrossist, og detailhandelsvirksomheden benævnt endetailhandel.Since it can be a big task for a manufacturing company to take care of the sale of goods to consumers, a manufacturing companycan choose to sell his goods on to other companies that can take care of that task, e.g. a wholesaletrading company or a retailtrading company. In Chapter 1, the wholesaletrading companyone was called a wholesaler , and the retailtrading companyone was called a retail .

I distributionskæden er det grossistens opgave at købe store partier varer hjem fra forskellige producenter. Det er grossistens opgave at have de varer, som deres kunder har brug for. Grossisternes kunder er detailhandlerne. De varer, grossisterne køber, er derfor varer, der passer ind i detsortiment, detailhandlerne har i deres butikker. På den måde bruger detailhandlerne ikke alt for meget tid på at kontakte og bestille varer hjem til butikkerne fra mange forskellige producenter.In the distribution chain, it is the wholesaler's task to buy home large batches of goods from various manufacturers. It is the wholesaler's job to have the goods that their customers need. wholesalers's customers are the retailers. The goods that wholesalersne buy are therefore goods that fit into what assortment the retailers have in their stores. In this way, the retailers do not spend too much time contacting and ordering goods to the stores from many different manufacturers.

10.2 Indirekte og direkte distribution10.2 Indirect and Direct Distribution

Der findes flere variationer af den generelle distributionskæde (figur 10.2). Der kan være tale om indirekte distribution eller direkte distribution.There are several variations of the general distribution chain (Figure 10.2). It can be indirect distribution or direct distribution.

Indirekte distributionIndirect distribution

I den generelle distributionskæde, er engroshandelsvirksomheder indsat som mellemled. Det kan dog også være en detailhandelsvirksomhed.In the general distribution chain, wholesale trading companies is used as an intermediary link. However, it can also be a retailtrading company.

Indirekte distribution betyder, at man har et eller flere led i distributionskæden mellem producent og forbruger.Indirect distribution means that you have one or more links in the distribution chain between producer and consumer.

I figur 10.6 kan du se, at en detailhandelsvirksomhed er indsat som et led mellemproduktionsvirksomhedog forbruger. Dette er et andet eksempel på indirekte distribution.In figure 10.6 you can see that a retail trading company is inserted as a link between manufacturing company and consumer. This is another example of indirect distribution.

En forholdsvis ny tendens er, at små producenter leverer deres varer direkte til detailhandelsvirksomheder. Et eksempel på sådan en virksomhed er den nordjyske melproducent Aurion. Virksomheden sælger deres varer gennem mange små gårdbutikker samt en række netbutikker. Disse sælger varerne videre til forbrugerne, og der er også her tale om indirekte distribution.A relatively new trend is for small producers to supply their goods directly to retailerstrading companies. An example of such a company is the North Jutland flour producer Aurion. The company sells its goods through many small farm shops as well as a number of online stores. These sell the goods on to consumers, and this is also indirect distribution.

Direkte distributionDirect distribution

Den variation af den generelle distributionskæde, hvor der ikke er nogen mellemhandler, hverken engroshandelsvirksomhed eller detailhandelsvirksomhed, kaldes direkte distribution.The variation of the general distribution chain in which there is no middleman, neither wholesaletrading company nor retailtrading company, is called direct distribution.

Direkte distribution betyder, at varen distribueres direkte fra producenten til forbrugeren, uden brug af mellemhandlere.Direct distribution means that the goods are distributed directly from the producer to the consumer, without the use of intermediaries.

Det er sjældent, at du som forbruger kan få adgang til at købe produkter direkte ved producenten. Der findes dog produktionsvirksomheder, som har åbnet egen webshop, hvor du kan få mulighed for at købe virksomhedens produkter.It is rare that you as a consumer can get access to buy products directly from the manufacturer. There is, however, manufacturing companies, which has opened its own webshop, where you can have the opportunity to buy the company's products.

Melproducenten aurion.dk har egen webshop, hvor forbrugeren kan købe produktet direkte ved producenten. Dette er også et eksempel på direkte distribution.The flour producer aurion.dk has its own webshop, where the consumer can buy the product directly from the producer. This is also an example of direct distribution.

Rundt omkring i landet ligger nogle gårdbutikker. Gårdbutikkerne sælger bla. de produkter, der dyrkes på egne markeder. Hvis du har prøvet at købe grøntsager i en gårdbutik, har du formentlig også prøvet at handle direkte med producenten.Around the country there are some farm shops. The farm shops sell, among other things, the products that are grown in own markets. If you've tried buying vegetables in a farm shop, you've probably also tried dealing directly with the producer.

Virksomheden Aarstiderne sælger måltidskasser til den almindelig forbruger. I kasserne ligger bl.a. forskellige grøntsager, der er dyrket på virksomhedens egne marker. Men det er ikke alle produkterne i måltidskasserne, der er fremstillet af Aarstiderne. Der er altså kun delvist tale om direkte distribution.The company Aarstiderne sells meal boxes to the general consumer. In the boxes are, among other things, various vegetables grown on the company's own fields. But not all the products in the meal boxes are manufactured by Aarstiderne. It is therefore only partly about direct distribution.

10.3 Channel marketing10.3 Channel marketing

For en virksomhed gælder det ofte om at få produkterne gjort tilgængelig for så mange kunder som muligt. Til det formål bruger de mange forskellige versioner af distributionskæden i et forsøg på at få produkterne placeret de steder, deres kunder handler. Når en virksomhed arbejder med distributionskæden på denne måde, kaldes det Channel Marketing.For a company, it is often about making the products available to as many customers as possible. To that end, they use many different versions of the distribution chain in an attempt to get the products to the places where their customers shop. When a company works with the distribution chain in this way, it is called Channel Marketing.

Channel marketingChannel marketing

Channel marketing er, når virksomheder aktivt anvender forskellige varianter af distributionskæden for at komme ud til forbrugerne.Channel marketing is when companies actively use different variants of the distribution chain to reach consumers.

Se mere om Channel marketing i afsnit12.3 Online promotion medier.See more about Channel marketing in section 12.3 Online promotion media.

Eksempel: Melproducenten AurionExample: The flour producer Aurion

Aurion producerer økologisk mel på diverse kornsorter. For at gøre sig synlig for de forbrugere, der søger økologiske produkter, er Aurion bevidste om, at deres produkter skal være synlige, der hvor forbrugerne køber deres økologiske varer. Forbrugerne opfatter produkterne som værende af høj kvalitet, og det forsøger Aurion at holde fast i ved at udvælge mellemhandlerne meget nøje.Aurion produces organic flour from various types of grain. In order to make itself visible to consumers looking for organic products, Aurion is aware that their products must be visible where consumers buy their organic goods. Consumers perceive the products as being of high quality, and Aurion tries to stick to this by selecting the middlemen very carefully.

Direct-to-consumer (D2C)Direct-to-consumer (D2C)

D2C betyder, at en virksomhed selv producerer og sælger sine produkter direkte til kunderne – uden at gå gennem butikker eller grossister.

Før i tiden solgte virksomheder deres varer til grossister og butikker, som så solgte dem videre til kunderne. Det gjorde det nemt at købe produkterne. For eksempel leverer Arla sine mejeriprodukter til butikker, hvor kunderne køber dem sammen med andredagligvarer.

I dag bruger kunderne mere tid online og undersøger produkter og priser. Derfor har mange virksomheder valgt at sælge direkte til kunderne via deres egne webshops – som et ekstra tilbud ved siden af den traditionelle måde.

Salomon sælger tøj og sko både via sin webshop og i sportsbutikker. Royal Copenhagen har i lang tid solgt hele sit udvalg i egne butikker og fokuserer nu mere på sin webshop. Nogle virksomheder, som Planet Nusa og Shaping New Tomorrow, sælger kun via egne webshops og butikker.

D2C-virksomheder fokuserer ofte på kvalitet og gode oplevelser frem for at sælge store mængder.D2C means that a company itself produces and sells its products directly to customers – without going through shops or wholesalers. In the past, companies sold their goods to wholesalers and shops, who then sold them on to customers. It made it easy to buy the products. For example, Arla delivers its dairy products to stores where customers buy them together with other daily groceries. Today, customers spend more time online researching products and prices. Therefore, many companies have chosen to sell directly to customers via their own webshops – as an additional offer alongside the traditional way. Salomon sells clothes and shoes both via its webshop and in sports shops. Royal Copenhagen has for a long time sold its entire selection in its own stores and is now focusing more on its webshop. Some companies, such as Planet Nusa and Shaping New Tomorrow, only sell via their own webshops and stores. D2C companies often focus on quality and good experiences rather than selling large quantities.

10.4 Kædetyper10.4 Chain types

Der er over de seneste år, sket en udvikling i retning af flere og større kæder. Denne udvikling er ikke bare sket i Danmark, men over hele verden. Nogle af formålene ved dette samarbejde er fx at spare penge på omkostningerne til drift, presse leverandører til lavere priser og give mulighed for mere synlig markedsføring.Over recent years, there has been a trend towards more and larger chains. This development has not just happened in Denmark, but all over the world. Some of the purposes of this collaboration are, for example, to save money on operating costs, pressure suppliers to lower prices and provide the opportunity for more visible marketing.

De forskellige kædetyper fremgår af figur 10.9, og dem gennemgår vi nærmere i dette afsnit.The different chain types appear in figure 10.9, and we review them in more detail in this section.

Forskellige typer kæderDifferent types of chains

Egentlige kæderActual chains

Egentlige kæder er alle ejet af den samme person eller virksomhed. Du kender også de egentlige kæder som kapitalkæder. Ejeren træffer alle beslutninger fra et hovedkontor, og butikkerne vil have det samme koncept. Der vil være fælles markedsføring, og hver butik ledes af en bestyrer, der er ansat af ejerne.Actual chains are all owned by the same person or company. You also know the actual chains as capital chains. The owner makes all decisions from a head office and the stores will have the same concept. There will be joint marketing and each store will be managed by a manager employed by the owners.

Du kender de egentlige kæder fra: Kvickly, Føtex, Sport 24 og Vero Moda. JOE and THE JUICE er også blevet en kæde, der blev startet med bare en enkelt juicebar i 2002, og nu har et meget stort antal juicebarer i mange forskellige lande.You know the actual chains from: Kvickly, Føtex, Sport 24 and Vero Moda. JOE and THE JUICE has also become a chain that was started with just a single juice bar in 2002 and now has a very large number of juice bars in many different countries.

Frivillige kæderVoluntary chains

De frivillige kæder adskiller sig egentlige kæder ved at være selvstændige butikker, der frivilligt har indgået en samarbejdsaftale om forskellige opgaver. Butikkerne har overvejende fælles markedsføring, men der kan være lokale forskelle. De enkelte butikker kan godt afvige fra det fælles koncept. Ejeren vælger selv, hvordan butikken ledes, men skal overvejende holde sig til konceptet.The voluntary chains differ from actual chains in that they are independent stores that have voluntarily entered into a cooperation agreement on various tasks. The stores mostly have joint marketing, but there may be local differences. The individual stores may deviate from the common concept. The owner chooses how the store is managed, but must predominantly stick to the concept.

Du kender de frivillige kæder fra: Skoringen, Mr og Bog &idé.You know the voluntary chains from: Skoringen, Mr and Bog & idé.

Franchise kæderFranchise chains

Denne kædetype er opstået på den måde, at en franchisetager køber rettigheden til at benytte franchisegiverens koncept.This type of chain has arisen in the way that a franchisee buys the right to use the franchisor's concept.

Der er altså en person, der gerne vil starte en virksomhed med et allerede kendt og afprøvet koncept.So there is a person who wants to start a business with an already known and tested concept.

Franchisetageren skal betale en leje af konceptet. Lejen er typisk en procentdel af omsætningen. Franchisetageren er selvstændig, men skal følge konceptet på samme måde som de egentlige kæder. Der er samme koncept i alle butikker, og markedsføringen er ens.The franchisee must pay a rent for the concept. The rent is typically a percentage of the turnover. The franchisee is independent, but must follow the concept in the same way as the actual chains. There is the same concept in all stores, and the marketing is the same.

Du kender franchisekæderne fra: Rema 1000, McDonald's og 7-Eleven.You know the franchise chains from: Rema 1000, McDonald's and 7-Eleven.

HybridkæderHybrid chains

Når vi taler om en hybridkæde, er der tale om en blanding af forskellige kæder. Det kunne fx være en kæde, hvor nogle butikker er ejet 100 % af ejeren - altså en egentlig kæde (kapitalkæde), og andre butikker er selvstændige butikker tilknyttet kæden - altså franchisebutikker.When we talk about a hybrid chain, we are talking about a mixture of different chains. It could, for example, be a chain where some stores are 100% owned by the owner - i.e. an actual chain (capital chain), and other stores are independent stores associated with the chain - i.e. franchise stores.

Mange danske detailhandelskæder har haft succes med at vokse ved at benytte sig af franchisebutikker. En af dem er Søstrene Grene: "Søstrene Grene kommer med et afprøvet konceptet, og franchisetagerne kender til gengæld markedet i det aktuelle land. Det er en god kombination", forklarer detailhandelsekspert Flemming Birch.Many Danish retailchains have been successful in growing by using franchise stores. One of them is Søstrene Grene: "Søstrene Grene comes with a tested concept, and the franchisees, in turn, know the market in the current country. It's a good combination", explains retails expert Flemming Birch.

Bestseller er et andet eksempel på en virksomhed, der skaber global vækst ved en kombination af egne butikker og franchisetagere.Bestseller is another example of a company that creates global growth through a combination of its own stores and franchisees.

10.5 Beliggenhed10.5 Beliggenhed

Beliggenheden spiller en stor rolle for alle typer fysiske virksomheder. Det er vigtigt at være placeret i nærheden af kunderne eller have mulighed for parkering, når kunderne kommer kørende til butikken.The location plays a big role for all types of physical businesses. It is important to be located near the customers or to have the option of parking when the customers drive to the store.

En benzinstation vil typisk være placeret, hvor der er meget trafik. Kiosken med blade, aviser og onlinespil ligger også placeret, hvor der kommer mange mennesker.A gas station will typically be located where there is a lot of traffic. The kiosk with magazines, newspapers and online games is also located where many people come.

Andre butikker placerer sig sammen i centre for bedre at kunne tiltrække kunderne, netop fordi butikkerne ligger samlet.Other stores are located together in centers in order to better attract customers, precisely because the stores are located together.

Ofte vil samme virksomhed benytte sig af mere end én form for beliggenhed. Tænk bare på de store supermarkedskæder, der ligger i udkanten af byen ved en stor parkeringsplads. Samme kæde kan også ligge centerorienteret, sammen med andre store butikker i et centermiljø, eller cityorienteret i midtbyen.Often the same company will use more than one type of location. Just think of the large supermarket chains located on the outskirts of the city by a large car park. The same chain can also be centrally located, together with other large stores in a central environment, or city-oriented in the city centre.

Detailhandelsbranchen udvikler sig markant i disse år henimod digitale løsninger i forskellig form. Det kan være webshoppen, der åbner en fysisk butik, eller et showroom. Det kan også være den fysiske butik, der udvider med en digital handelsplatform i form af en webshop.The retails industry is developing significantly in these years towards digital solutions in various forms. It can be the webshop that opens a physical store or a showroom. It can also be the physical store that expands with a digital trading platform in the form of a webshop.

Disse former for Channel Marketing (markedsføring af sit produkt på flere kanaler) har også betydning for placering af butikker. Det betyder, at den fysiske butik ikke nødvendigvis skal være cityorienteret med betaling af høj husleje, hvis kunden først har fundet varen på nettet og bare vil forbi for at se den i en fysisk udgave inden købet. Channel Marketing behandles nærmere i kaptitel13. Promotion.These forms of Channel Marketing (marketing one's product on several channels) are also important for the location of stores. This means that the physical store does not necessarily have to be city-oriented with payment of high rent, if the customer has first found the item online and just wants to stop by to see it in a physical version before the purchase. Channel Marketing is dealt with in more detail in chapter 13. Promotion.

Søstrene Grene har nogle stærke sider ved, at de har butikker i gågader og centre, hvor der færdes mange mennesker.Søstrene Grene has some strong points in that they have shops in pedestrian streets and centers where many people move.

Til gengæld skal de ofte betale en høj husleje, og de bliver nødt til at have store butikslokaler til deres brede produktudvalg. Det er svage sider ved deres placeringer.On the other hand, they often have to pay a high rent, and they have to have large shop premises for their wide range of products. These are weak sides of their placements.

De kan udnytte, at forbrugerne i højere grad efterspørger muligheden for at købe forskelligartede produkter, uden at skulle hele byen rundt. Det taler for placeringer i gågader og storcentre.They can take advantage of the fact that consumers are increasingly demanding the opportunity to buy a variety of products without having to go all the way around town. This speaks for locations in pedestrian streets and shopping centres.

Til gengæld er det en trussel, at de store byer gør det sværere at komme helt ind til centrum i bil. Der ses også en tendens til, at der kommer færre mennesker i gågaderne, fordi nethandel tager en stadig større del af omsætningen.On the other hand, it is a threat that the big cities make it more difficult to get right into the center by car. There is also a tendency for fewer people to come to the pedestrian streets, because online shopping takes an increasingly large part of the turnover.
