Lakrids by Bülow B2BLakrids by Bülow B2B

Lakrids by Bülow sælger deres produkter ved brug af forskellige kanaler. I denne case, og dokumentationsopgave, er der fokus påB2B-salget.Licorice by Bülow sells its products using various channels. In this case and documentation assignment, the focus is on B2B sales.

Ideen bag virksomheden Lakrids by Bülow, der blev etableret i 2007, er at fremstille håndlavet lakrids som en delikatesse. Der er stort fokus på de bedste råvarer og specielle krydderier. Der bruges ingen tilsætningsstoffer i de håndlavede lakridser, som sælges i eksklusiv emballage.The idea behind the company Lakrids by Bülow, which was established in 2007, is to produce handmade licorice as a delicacy. There is a great focus on the best raw materials and special spices. No additives are used in the handmade liquorices, which are sold in exclusive packaging.

I starten blev lakridsen solgt fra et lille lakridskogeri i Svaneke på Bornholm og fra en bod i Tivoli i København. Stifteren af virksomheden, Johan Bülow, etablerede i 2008 en mindre produktion i Taastrup efterhånden som salget steg. To år senere blev produktionen flyttet til Hvidovre, hvor virksomhedens hovedkontor og produktion fortsat ligger.In the beginning, licorice was sold from a small licorice factory in Svaneke on Bornholm and from a stall in Tivoli in Copenhagen. The founder of the company, Johan Bülow, established a smaller production in Taastrup in 2008 as sales increased. Two years later, production was moved to Hvidovre, where the company's head office and production are still located.

I dag er Lakrids by Bülow også på eksportmarkederne hos mere end 2.000 forhandlere i 36 lande, og har også i alt 40 koncept butikker. Prisen på produktet ligger på ca.70-80 kr. for 100 gram. Andelen af omsætningen, som sælges på hjemmemarkedet, lå i 2023 på lidt over 30 procent.Today, Lakrids by Bülow is also on the export markets with more than 2,000 dealers in 36 countries, and also has a total of 40 concept stores. The price of the product is approximately DKK 70-80 for 100 grams. The share of revenue that is sold on the domestic market was in 2023 at just over 30 percent.

I begyndelsen af 2024 opnåede Lakrids by Bülow B Corp-certificering, hvilket gør dem til den første premium konfekturevirksomhed i Norden, der opnår denne certificering. Dette afspejler virksomhedens kerneværdier og engagement i høje sociale og miljømæssige standarder.At the beginning of 2024, Lakrids by Bülow achieved B Corp certification, making them the first premium confectionery company in the Nordics to achieve this certification. This reflects the company's core values ​​and commitment to high social and environmental standards.

Lakrids by Bülow sælger altså deres produkter ved brug af forskellige kanaler. I dokumentationsopgaven skal du arbejde med B2B, og der vil være fokus på handelsuddannelsen.Likrids by Bülow therefore sell their products using different channels. In the documentation task, you must work with B2B, and there will be a focus on the business education.

Internationale aktiviteterInternationale aktiviteter

I dag er Lakrids by Bülow er også på eksportmarkederne hos mere end 2.000 forhandlere i 36 lande, og har også i alt 40 koncept butikker. E-Commerce delen er også styrket betydeligt i de senere år og i dag sælges der til over 50 lande gennem deres webshop.Today, Lakrids by Bülow is also on the export markets with more than 2,000 dealers in 36 countries, and also has a total of 40 concept stores. The E-Commerce part has also been strengthened considerably in recent years and today sales are made to over 50 countries through their webshop.

VirksomhedensCEO, Fredrik Nilsson, har erfaring med internationalisering af andre virksomheder samt haft lederposter i Nestlé og Nespresso, og bestyrelsesformand Tue Mantoni har også erfaring med internationalisering af bl.a. Soundboks ApS.The company's CEO, Fredrik Nilsson, has experience with the internationalization of other companies and held management positions in Nestlé and Nespresso, and chairman of the board Tue Mantoni also has experience with the internationalization of e.g. Soundboks ApS.

I dag er koncernen i høj grad en Direct-to-Consumer-forretning, hvor e-handlen er den vigtigste salgsarena. Det er meget givende, at vi har været i stand til at opretholde en solid tocifret vækst over de senere årToday, the group is largely a Direct-to-Consumer business, where e-commerce is the most important sales arena. It is very rewarding that we have been able to maintain solid double-digit growth over recent years

Fredrik Nilsson, CEOFredrik Nilsson, CEO

Virksomhedens lakridser spises og serveres mange steder. Verdens største flyselskab, Emirates, har siden 2017 serveret Lakrids fra Lakrids by Bülow på ”første klasse”. I alt 3,3 millioner lakridskugler serveres i de højere luftlag hvert år. Flyselskabet har cirka 77 millioner passagerer om året.The company's liquorices are eaten and served in many places. The world's largest airline, Emirates, has served Lakrids from Lakrids by Bülow in "first class" since 2017. A total of 3.3 million licorice balls are served in the upper air layers each year. The airline has approximately 77 million passengers per year.

Virksomheden ønsker at åbne flere konceptbutikker på udenlandske markeder i fremtiden. Især Tyskland er interessant, da det er et marked i vækst hvor virksomheden i forvejen har mange butikker. Det er dog ikke ligetil bare at åbne butikker:The company wants to open more concept stores in foreign markets in the future. Germany in particular is interesting, as it is a growing market where the company already has many stores. However, it is not straightforward to just open stores:

Vi har en lang liste med muligheder, og det er mere en regel end undtagelse, at jeg er der. For det er en kombination af hjerte og hjerne. Vi har brug for en lokation, hvor der er nok mennesker, men den branding-værdi, vi ser, skal også være tilpas god for os. Lejen skal også være i orden, og det er ofte det svære.We have a long list of possibilities and it is more the rule than the exception that I am there. Because it is a combination of heart and brain. We need a location where there are enough people, but the branding value we see must also be good enough for us. The rent must also be in order, and that is often the difficult part.

Fredrik Nilsson, CEOFredrik Nilsson, CEO

Økonomisk udviklingEconomic development

I juli 2016 solgte Johan Bülow en del af virksomheden til den svenske kapitalfond Valedo, der også ejer Joe & The Juice. Johan Bülow ejer fortsat en mindre del af virksomheden, mens Valedo ejer 75 procent af virksomheden. Man betalte i 2016 cirka 470 millioner kroner for den andel. I den forbindelse blev Johan Bülow kreativ direktør for virksomheden. Siden er virksomhedens internationalisering fortsat og i 2019 skiftede virksomheden navn til Lakrids by Bülow som led i en redefinering af brandet i en mere bæredygtig og international retning. Emballagen produceres fx nu i 100 procent genbrugt plast.In July 2016, Johan Bülow sold part of the company to the Swedish capital fund Valedo, which also owns Joe & The Juice. Johan Bülow still owns a smaller part of the company, while Valedo owns 75 percent of the company. In 2016, approximately DKK 470 million was paid for that share. In this connection, Johan Bülow became creative director of the company. Since then, the company's internationalization has continued and in 2019 the company changed its name to Lakrids by Bülow as part of a redefinition of the brand in a more sustainable and international direction. The packaging, for example, is now produced in 100 percent recycled plastic.

På trods af en stigende omsætning og bruttofortjeneste, har Lakrids by Bülow svært ved at generere et overskud. Om resultatet for regnskabsåret i 2024 udtalte CEO Fredrik Nilsson:Despite an increasing turnover and gross profit, Lakrids by Bülow is finding it difficult to generate a profit. About the result for the financial year in 2024, CEO Fredrik Nilsson stated:

Situationen for konsumentprodukter generelt i Norden og Tyskland har været udfordrende. Flere af vores forhandlere, særligt i Norge og Danmark, er gået konkurs, og vi har været ramt af valutaudsving i Sverige og Norge, som gør, at forbrugerne ikke har haft samme købekraft som tidligere. Omvendt kan vi se, at vores koncept fungerer, så selvom vi havde ønsket mere vækst, mener vi, at det ser positivt ud fremover.The situation for consumer products in general in the Nordics and Germany has been challenging. Several of our dealers, especially in Norway and Denmark, have gone bankrupt, and we have been affected by currency fluctuations in Sweden and Norway, which means that consumers have not had the same purchasing power as before. On the other hand, we can see that our concept is working, so even though we would have liked more growth, we believe that it looks positive going forward.
