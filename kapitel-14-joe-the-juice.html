JOE & THE JUICEJOE & THE JUICE

Danskerne har for alvor fået smag for friskpresset juice og smoothies, og mange nye juicebarer skyder op. Den første og største kæde i den danske branche er JOE & THE JUICE.The Danes have really developed a taste for freshly squeezed juice and smoothies, and many new juice bars are popping up. The first and largest chain in the Danish industry is JOE & THE JUICE.

BaggrundBackground

Kasper Basse åbnede sin første JOE & THE JUICE juicebar i 2002.Kasper Basse opened his first JOE & THE JUICE juice bar in 2002.

I 2025 har kæden 400 juicebarer verden over, med flest i Danmark. Kæden satser stort på det internationale marked, især i USA, hvor den har åbnet juicebarer i byer som New York, Florida, Chicago og San Diego.In 2025, the chain will have 400 juice bars worldwide, with the most in Denmark. The chain is betting big on the international market, especially in the United States, where it has opened juice bars in cities such as New York, Florida, Chicago and San Diego.

JOE & THE JUICE har fået opmærksomhed for deres samarbejde med kendte personligheder. Sammen med NBA-stjernen Kevin Durant lancerede kæden en premium steak sandwich i februar 2025. I juni samme år annoncerede de et partnerskab med tennisspilleren Novak Djokovic.JOE & THE JUICE have gained attention for their collaborations with famous personalities. Together with NBA star Kevin Durant, the chain launched a premium steak sandwich in February 2025. In June of the same year, they announced a partnership with tennis player Novak Djokovic.

KonceptKoncept

JOE & THE JUICE kombinerer sundhed og oplevelser. De tilbyder frugt- og grøntsagsjuice, kaffe og sandwiches, som kan nydes til høj musik.JOE & THE JUICE combines health and experiences. They offer fruit and vegetable juices, coffee and sandwiches, which can be enjoyed with loud music.

Medarbejderne, der er uddannet på virksomhedens Juice Academy, udstråler energi og overskud. Alt laves fra bunden af friske råvarer i en dynamisk atmosfære.The employees, trained at the company's Juice Academy, exude energy and enthusiasm. Everything is made from scratch with fresh ingredients in a dynamic atmosphere.


Ejerskab og økonomiOwnership and finances

I 2024 steg salget med over 300 millioner kroner. Omsætningen rundede 3 milliarder kroner og virksomheden havde ved årets slutning 2.000 ansatte.In 2024, sales increased by over DKK 300 million. The turnover reached DKK 3 billion and the company had 2,000 employees at the end of the year.

På trods af de mange nye juicebarer, havde kæden i 2024 et overskud på 150 mio. kr.Despite the many new juice bars, the chain had a profit of DKK 150 million in 2024. DKK

Virksomheden er i dag primært ejet af kapitalfonde, og dens vækststrategi er baseret på egen drift af juicebarerne frem for vækst gennem et franchisekoncept.The company is today primarily owned by capital funds, and its growth strategy is based on own operation of the juice bars rather than growth through a franchise concept.

BranchenThe industry

JOE & THE JUICE er en del af cafe- og restaurantbranchen, der i Danmark består af en lang række mindre spillere.JOE & THE JUICE is part of the cafe and restaurant industry, which in Denmark consists of a large number of smaller players.

Der er omkring 3.500 cafeer og barer i Danmark, og antallet er steget pænt i de senere år. Cafeerne tager markedsandele fra egentlige restauranter, og take-away er i vækst.There are around 3,500 cafes and bars in Denmark, and the number has risen nicely in recent years. Cafes are taking market share from actual restaurants, and take-away is growing.

De vigtigste spillere på cafemarkedet er Joe & The Juice, Espresso House-kæden, Café Vivaldi og Starbucks.The main players in the cafe market are Joe & The Juice, the Espresso House chain, Café Vivaldi and Starbucks.

Antallet af juicebarer er stadig meget lille i Danmark - ca. 600 på landsplan. Joe & The Juice er den dominerende spiller inden for juicebarer med klart den største markedsandel.The number of juice bars is still very small in Denmark - approx. 600 nationwide. Joe & The Juice is the dominant player in juice bars with by far the largest market share.
