1.1.1 Produktionsvirksomheder1.1.1 Production companies

Der findes to typer produktionsvirksomheder:There are two types of manufacturing companies:

  • RåvareproducenterRaw material producers
  • FærdigvareproducenterFinished goods manufacturers

Råvareproducenten udvinder råvarer, mens næste led i distributionskæden, nemlig færdigvareproducenter forarbejder råvarer og hjælpematerialer til færdigvarer.The raw material producer extracts raw materials, while the next link in the distribution chain, namely finished goods producers, processes raw materials and auxiliary materials into finished goods.

ProduktionsvirksomhedProduction company

Enproduktionsvirksomhedudvinder eller fremstiller selv de varer, den sælger.A manufacturing company extracts or manufactures itself the goods it sells.

Eksempel: RåvareproducentExample: Commodity producer

Råvareproducenter kan være landmænd, der leverer råmælk til mejerier, eller grusgraven, der udvinder ler og sand til fremstilling af mursten.Raw material producers can be farmers who supply raw milk to dairies, or the gravel pit that extracts clay and sand to make bricks.

Eksempel: ProduktionsvirksomhedExample: Manufacturing company

Arla, LEGO og Bang & Olufsen er eksempler på produktionsvirksomheder, som indkøber forskellige råvarer og komponenter hos deres leverandører, der betegnes råvareproducenter og underleverandører.Arla, LEGO and Bang & Olufsen are examples of production companies that purchase various raw materials and components from their suppliers, which are referred to as raw material producers and sub-suppliers.

Arla indkøber råmælken hos landmændene og forarbejder den til skummetmælk, ost og andre mejeriprodukter.Arla buys raw milk from farmers and processes it into skimmed milk, cheese and other dairy products.

I en produktionsvirksomhed er der følgende tre former for varelagre:In a manufacturing company, there are the following three types of inventories:

  • RåvarelagerRaw material warehouse
  • Varer under fremstilling i produktionsafdelingenGoods under production in the production department
  • FærdigvarelagerFinished goods warehouse

Produktionsvirksomheder varierer meget i størrelse fra de helt små virksomheder til de meget store industrivirksomheder. Som eksempler på store danske produktionsvirksomheder kan man foruden Novo Nordisk nævne A.P.Møller - Mærsk, Vestas Wind Systems, Carlsberg, Danfoss, Grundfos, Arla Foods, LEGO og Pandora.Manufacturing companies vary greatly in size from very small companies to very large industrial companies. As examples of large Danish manufacturing companies, in addition to Novo Nordisk, A.P. Møller - Mærsk, Vestas Wind Systems, Carlsberg, Danfoss, Grundfos, Arla Foods, LEGO and Pandora can be mentioned.

En produktionsvirksomheds markedsopgave er bl.a. at:A production company's market task is, among other things, to:

  • Udvikle interessante produkter til kunderneDevelop interesting products for customers
  • Fremstille produkterne til lavest muligeomkostningeri forhold til den ønskede kvalitetProduce the products at the lowest possible costs in relation to the desired quality
  • Levere produkterne til kunderne i rette mængde og til rette tid.Deliver the products to customers in the right quantity and at the right time.

1.1.2 Handelsvirksomheder1.1.2 Trading companies

Enhandelsvirksomheder kendetegnet ved, at den sælger de samme varer til kunderne, som den har indkøbt hos leverandørerne, uden egentlig forarbejdning.A trading company is characterized by the fact that it sells the same goods to customers that it has purchased from suppliers, without actual processing.

Der findes to slags handelsvirksomheder, nemlig:There are two types of trading companies, namely:

  • EngrosvirksomhederWholesale companies
  • DetailvirksomhederRetail businesses

En engrosvirksomhed eller en grossist er en handelsvirksomhed, der videresælger varer til andre virksomheder. Varerne indkøbes hos forskellige producenter i store mængder, hvorefter de videresælges i mindre mængder til forskellige detailforretninger. Da både grossister og detailforretninger er virksomheder, kaldes det marked, grossister sælger varer på, for B2B-markedet (Business to Business).A wholesale company or a wholesaler is a trading company that resells goods to other companies. The goods are purchased from various manufacturers in large quantities, after which they are resold in smaller quantities to various retail outlets. Since both wholesalers and retailers are businesses, the market in which wholesalers sell goods is called the B2B market (Business to Business).

HandelsvirksomhedTrading company

En handelsvirksomhed (engrosvirksomhed og detailvirksomhed) indkøber færdigvarer, som den videresælger til sine kunder uden at ændre varerne.A trading company (wholesale and retail) purchases finished goods, which it resells to its customers without changing the goods.

EngrosvirksomhederWholesale companies

Engrosvirksomhedens markedsopgave er bl.a. at:The wholesale company's market task is, among other things, to:

  • Finde de rigtige produkter hos de rigtige producenterFind the right products from the right manufacturers
  • Sammensætte et godt produktsortiment og servicere detailleddetPut together a good product range and service the retail section
  • Levere produkterne i de mængder, kunderne efterspørger.Deliver the products in the quantities the customers demand.

Der findes engrosvirksomheder inden for mange forskellige brancher, men disse virksomheder er ofte ikke særligt kendte af den almindelige dansker, da engrosvirksomheder normalt ikke har nogen kontakt med den endelige forbruger.There are wholesale companies in many different industries, but these companies are often not well known to ordinary Danes, as wholesale companies usually have no contact with the final consumer.

Eksempel: EngrosvirksomhederExample: Wholesale companies

Ahlsell (tidligere Sanistål) er leverandør af installationsprodukter, værktøjer og maskiner til professionelle i bryggebranchen, mens Dagrofa Foodservice er en ledende totalleverandør til foodservicesektoren i Danmark.Ahlsell (formerly Sanistål) is supplier of installation products, tools and machines for professionals in the brewing industry, while Dagrofa Foodservice is a leading total supplier to the foodservice sector in Denmark.

DetailvirksomhederRetail businesses

En detailvirksomhed eller en detaillist er enhandelsvirksomhed, der sælger varer direkte til forbrugerne. Detailvirksomheder indkøber varerne direkte hos producenterne eller gennem grossistvirksomheder. Når detailforretninger sælger til forbrugere, kaldes det marked, detailforretninger sælger varer på, for B2C-markedet (Business to Consumer).A retail company or a retail list is a trading company that sells goods directly to consumers. Retail companies purchase the goods directly from the manufacturers or through wholesalers. When retail stores sell to consumers, the market in which retail stores sell goods is called the B2C (Business to Consumer) market.

Detailvirksomhedens markedsopgave er stort set den samme som engrosvirksomhedens. En vigtig forskel er dog, at detailvirksomhedens kunder er private forbrugere. Virksomhedens beliggenhed har derfor stor betydning for afsætningsmulighederne.The market task of the retail company is largely the same as that of the wholesale company. An important difference, however, is that the retail company's customers are private consumers. The location of the company is therefore of great importance for the sales opportunities.

Eksempel: DetailvirksomhederExample: Retail businesses

Supermarkeder, tøjbutikker, byggemarkeder og møbelforretninger er eksempler på detailforretninger. Detailhandelen er i mange tilfælde organiseret i kæder som fx Imerco, Søstrene Grene, Intersport og Skoringen.Supermarkets, clothing stores, hardware stores and furniture stores are examples of retail stores. In many cases, the retail trade is organized in chains such as Imerco, Søstrene Grene, Intersport and Skoringen.

Handelsvirksomheden har kun ét varelager. Varelageret kan befinde sig på hylderne i butikken og/eller i lagerlokaler. Varerne tages ud af varelageret, når de bliver solgt og udleveret til kunderne. Der foregår ikke nogen bearbejdning af varerne, mens de er i virksomheden.The trading company has only one warehouse. The inventory can be on the shelves in the store and/or in warehouses. The goods are taken out of the warehouse when they are sold and delivered to the customers. No processing of the goods takes place while they are in the company.

Udvikling i handelsstrukturenDevelopment in the trade structure

Der er to tendenser i handelsstrukturen:There are two trends in the trade structure:

  • Dagligvarehandelen er præget af større enheder som lavprisvarehuse, stormagasiner og detailkæder, hvilket i høj grad har gjort grossistleddet mindre nødvendigt.The grocery trade is characterized by larger units such as discount warehouses, department stores and retail chains, which has largely made the wholesaler less necessary.
  • e-handel har betydet, at varer som tøj, vin, bøger og musik i stigende grad sælges direkte fra grossist eller importør til forbrugeren uden om detailleddet. Mange grossister fungerer derfor også som detaillister.e-commerce has meant that goods such as clothes, wine, books and music are increasingly sold directly from the wholesaler or importer to the consumer, bypassing the retail stage. Many wholesalers therefore also act as retail lists.

E-butikker gør det muligt at spare tid, finde information og handle uafhængigt af åbningstider. Fysiske butikker fungerer ofte som showrooms, hvor kunder får information og service. I praksis supplerer netbutikker og fysiske butikker hinanden, og virksomheder bruger ofte begge salgskanaler.E-shops make it possible to save time, find information and shop regardless of opening hours. Physical stores often act as showrooms where customers get information and service. In practice, online stores and physical stores complement each other, and companies often use both sales channels.

1.1.3 Servicevirksomheder1.1.3 Service companies

Enservicevirksomhedfremstiller og sælger serviceydelser. De kaldes også tjenesteydelser. En servicevirksomhed producerer og forhandler ikke varer, men udfører forskellige arbejdsopgaver for sine kunder/klienter.A service company produces and sells services. They are also called services. A service company does not produce and sell goods, but performs various tasks for its customers/clients.

En stor del af den offentlige sektor består af servicevirksomheder, fx sygehuse, skoler, børnehaver, DSB og Banedanmark.A large part of the public sector consists of service companies, e.g. hospitals, schools, kindergartens, DSB and Banedanmark.

Som eksempler på servicevirksomheder i den private sektor kan nævnes frisørsaloner, reklamebureauer, revisorer, advokater, tandlæger, fitnesscentre, forlystelsesparker, banker og forsikringsselskaber.Examples of service companies in the private sector include hairdressing salons, advertising agencies, accountants, lawyers, dentists, gyms, amusement parks, banks and insurance companies.

Servicevirksomhedens kunder kan være offentlige eller private virksomheder samt private forbrugere. En servicevirksomheds ydelser kan ikke fremstilles og lægges på lager. Det betyder, at virksomheden skal have tilstrækkelig kapacitet til at opfylde kundernes behov præcis på de tidspunkter, hvor de opstår. Det er derfor svært at bedømme serviceydelsens kvalitet på forhånd, da produktion og forbrug foregår samtidigt.The service company's customers can be public or private companies as well as private consumers. A service company's services cannot be manufactured and placed on inventory. This means that the company must have sufficient capacity to meet customers' needs exactly at the times when they arise. It is therefore difficult to judge the quality of the service in advance, as production and consumption take place simultaneously.

Antallet af servicevirksomheder og antallet af beskæftigede i servicesektoren har været kraftigt stigende i Danmark i løbet af de seneste 10-15 år. Det skyldes, at forbrugerne i stigende omfang efterspørger serviceydelser som underholdning, rejser, rådgivning og pleje. Det er sket i takt med, at informationssamfundet har afløst industrisamfundet, hvor der i dag er stort fokus på information, viden og en voksende servicesektor.The number of service companies and the number of people employed in the service sector has been increasing strongly in Denmark over the past 10-15 years. This is because consumers increasingly demand services such as entertainment, travel, advice and care. This has happened in tandem with the fact that the information society has replaced the industrial society, where today there is a great focus on information, knowledge and a growing service sector.

ServicevirksomhedService company

En servicevirksomhed sælger serviceydelser, dvs. at der ikke er tale om et håndgribeligt produkt.A service company sells services, i.e. that it is not a tangible product.

1.1.4 Blandede virksomheder1.1.4 Mixed companies

Nogle virksomheder har aktiviteter inden for flere områder. Det gælder fx mange frisørsaloner, som ud over at sælge serviceydelsen "frisørarbejde" også sælger fx shampoo og voks. På samme måde sælger mange fitnesscentre ved siden af serviceydelsen "motion" også fx sportstøj og energidrikke. Der er her tale om virksomheder, som både har service- og handelsaktiviteter. Virksomheder, der har aktiviteter inden for flere områder, betegnes blandede virksomheder.Some companies have activities in several areas. This applies, for example, to many hairdressing salons, which in addition to selling the service "hairdressing" also sell e.g. shampoo and wax. In the same way, many fitness centers also sell e.g. sportswear and energy drinks in addition to the "exercise" service. These are companies that have both service and trading activities. Companies that have activities in several areas are called mixed companies.

Mange håndværksvirksomheder som fx murere og tømrere sælger ved siden af serviceydelsen "reparationsarbejde" også fast ejendom, som de har opført for egen regning med salg for øje. Det er virksomheder, som både har service- og produktionsaktiviteter.Many craft companies, such as bricklayers and carpenters, in addition to the "repair work" service, also sell real estate, which they have built at their own expense with a view to selling. These are companies that have both service and production activities.

De fleste bagere har til deres bageri tilknyttet en bagerbutik, hvor der også sælges mælk og smør. Der er her tale om en virksomhed, som både har produktions- og handelsaktiviteter.Most bakers have a bakery attached to their bakery, where milk and butter are also sold. This is a company that has both production and trading activities.

A.P. Møller – Mærsk er en global virksomhed med en vifte af aktiviteter primært indenfor transport oglogistik, herunder containerskibsfart og terminaldrift.A. P. Møller – Maersk is a global company with a range of activities primarily within transport and logistics, including container shipping and terminal operations.

Man skelner mellem offentlige virksomheder og private virksomheder. Offentlige virksomheder er ejet af staten, regionerne eller kommunerne. De fleste offentlige virksomheder er servicevirksomheder som fx sygehuse, skoler og børnehaver. Offentlige virksomheder har som formål at dække nogle vigtige behov i samfundet. Hvis forbrugerne selv betaler helt eller delvist for en offentlig ydelse, taler man om brugerbetaling.A distinction is made between public companies and private companies. Public companies are owned by the state, the regions or the municipalities. Most public companies are service companies such as hospitals, schools and kindergartens. Public companies aim to cover some important needs in society. The operation of public enterprises is paid for in whole or in part through taxes. If consumers themselves pay in whole or in part for a public service, this is referred to as user payment.

Private virksomheder skal skabe overskud til virksomhedens ejer/ejere. En virksomhed kan juridisk være organiseret på flere forskellige måder. Man taler om virksomhedens ejerform eller om virksomhedsform.Private companies must generate profits for the company's owner/owners. A company can be legally organized in several different ways. We talk about the company's ownership or corporate form.

EgenkapitalEquity

Virksomhedens ejer har forhåbentlig selv sparet nogle penge op, som kan indskydes i forretningen. Dette kapitalindskud kaldes egenkapital. Egenkapitaler den kapital, ejerne har stillet til rådighed for virksomheden.Equity is the capital that the owners have made available to the company.

1.4.5 Momsafregning1.4.5 VAT settlement

En virksomhed skal altså lægge 25 % imomsoven i salgsprisen, når den sælger en vare eller en serviceydelse. Man kalder denne moms forsalgsmoms. Salgsmomsen skal senere indbetales til SKAT.A company must therefore add 25% VAT on top of the sales price when it sells a product or a service. This VAT is called sales VAT. The sales tax must later be paid to SKAT.

Når en virksomhed køber en vare eller en serviceydelse, bliver der på tilsvarende vis lagt 25 % til købsprisen. Denne moms kaldeskøbsmoms, og den vil senere blive godtgjort af SKAT. Det betyder med andre ord, at det er forskellen mellem salgsmomsen og købsmomsen, der skal afregnes med SKAT.When a company buys a product or a service, 25% is similarly added to the purchase price. This VAT is called purchase VAT, and it will later be reimbursed by SKAT. In other words, this means that it is the difference between the sales VAT and the purchase VAT that must be settled with VAT.
