Enhandelsvirksomheder kendetegnet ved, at den sælger nøjagtig de samme varer til kunderne, som den har købt ved leverandørerne. Der findes to slags handelsvirksomheder, nemligA trading company is characterized by the fact that it sells exactly the same goods to customers as it has bought from suppliers. There are two types of trading companies viz

  • engrosvirksomheder ogwholesale companies and
  • detailvirksomheder.retail businesses.

Engrosvirksomheders kunder er andre virksomheder, mens detailvirksomheders kunder er den endelige forbruger. Varestrømmen i de to typer handelsvirksomheder er dog ens. Varestrømmen begynder ved virksomhedens leverandører og passerer gennem virksomheden for til sidst at slutte hos kunderne. Varestrømmen er vist i figur 5.1.The customers of wholesale companies are other companies, while the customers of retail companies are the final consumer. The flow of goods in the two types of trading companies is, however, the same. The flow of goods begins at the company's suppliers and passes through the company to finally end at the customers. The flow of goods is shown in Figure 5.1.

VarestrømProduct flow

Varestrømmen viser varens vej fra virksomhedens leverandør til virksomhedens kunde.The product flow shows the product's path from the company's supplier to the company's customer.

Indkøbs- og lagerfunktionen samt salgsfunktionen løser forskellige opgaver i forbindelse med dennevarestrøm. Hvis virksomheden skal have en effektivlogistik, er det nødvendigt med et tæt samarbejde og udveksling af informationer mellem de enkelte funktioner. Ud fra et helhedssyn er det fx ikke effektivt, at indkøbsafdelingen er gode til at købe varer hjem til en god pris, hvis varerne ikke kan sælges af salgsafdelingen. Det er på samme måde heller ikke effektivt, at indkøbsafdelingen indkøber varer i store partier for at opnå kvantumsrabat, hvis varerne så skal ligge meget lang tid på lager, før de bliver solgt.The purchasing and storage function as well as the sales function solve various tasks in connection with this flow of goods. If the company is to have an effective logistics, close cooperation and exchange of information between the individual functions is necessary. From an overall view, it is not effective, for example, that the purchasing department is good at buying goods home at a good price if the goods cannot be sold by the sales department. In the same way, it is also not efficient for the purchasing department to purchase goods in large batches in order to obtain a quantity discount, if the goods then have to sit for a very long time on inventory before they are sold.

Den eksterne transport til kunden afhænger af, om der er tale om en detail- eller engrosvirksomhed. I en traditionel detailbutik (B2C) transporterer kunden normalt selv varerne hjem, hvorimod grossister (B2B) ofte anvender eksterne distributører (vognmænd og speditører).The external transport to the customer depends on whether it is a retail or wholesale business. In a traditional retail store (B2C), the customer usually transports the goods home themselves, whereas wholesalers (B2B) often use external distributors (truckers and freight forwarders).

Den interne transportaf varer afhænger også af, om der er tale om en detail- eller engrosvirksomhed. As described in section 1.1.2 Trading companies, the trading company has only one type of warehouse, namely a finished goods warehouse. In a retail store, there will often be an internal transport of the goods from an inventory in the 'backroom' to the shelves in the store.

Det viste eksempel begynder med, at virksomhedens salgsafdeling modtager en såkaldt ekstern ordre (salgsordre) fra en kunde (1). Salgsafdelingen undersøger straks, om varerne kan leveres direkte fra færdigvarelageret (2). Hvis dette ikke er muligt, går der en intern ordre (rekvisition) til virksomhedens indkøbsafdeling (3), som straks afgiver en ekstern ordre (indkøbsordre) til virksomhedens leverandører (4).The example shown begins with the company's sales department receiving a so-called external order (sales order) from a customer (1). The sales department immediately investigates whether the goods can be delivered directly from the finished goods warehouse (2). If this is not possible, an internal order (requisition) goes to the company's purchasing department (3), which immediately issues an external order (purchase order) to the company's suppliers (4).

Når de bestilte varer modtages fra leverandørerne, indgår de på virksomhedens færdigvarelager (5). Når varerne sælges, distribueres de til kunden (6).When the ordered goods are received from the suppliers, they are included in the company's finished goods inventory (5). When the goods are sold, they are distributed to the customer (6).

The reason is that the finance department does not directly contribute to safe planning, implementation and control of an efficient flow of goods from the suppliers through the company and out to the customers.

Efterhånden, som en virksomhed vokser, øges behovet for at fordele arbejdsopgaverne mellem forskellige afdelinger. En sådan specialisering er ofte en forudsætning for at opnå økonomisk effektivitet. I afsnit 5.3.1 ser vi derfor på de klassiske principper for arbejdsdeling mellem virksomhedens afdelinger.As a company grows, the need to distribute work tasks between different departments increases. Such specialization is often a prerequisite for obtaining economic efficiency. In section 5.3.1 we therefore look at the classic principles for division of labor between the company's departments.

En virksomhed kan også prøve at forbedre sin økonomiske effektivitet ved at overdrage (udlicitere) arbejdsopgaver til en anden virksomhed. Et eksempel er enproduktionsvirksomhed, der selv har lastbiler og chauffører. Et alternativ kan være at udlicitere transportfunktionen til en ekstern vognmand, der måske kan håndtere opgaven både bedre og billigere. En sådan udlicitering betegnesoutsourcing, hvilket uddybes i afsnit 5.3.2.A company can also try to improve its financial efficiency by handing over (outsourcing) work tasks to another company. An example is a manufacturing company that has its own trucks and drivers. An alternative could be to outsource the transport function to an external haulier who might be able to handle the task both better and cheaper. Such outsourcing is called outsourcing, which is explained in more detail in section 5.3.2.

Store virksomheder opretter ofte en særskilt logistikafdeling, der skal sørge for, at de øvrige afdelinger "spiller sammen". Mindre og mellemstore virksomheder ansætter måske en enkelt medarbejder, der er ansvarlig for logistikken. I små virksomheder er det normalt ikke nødvendigt med en særskilt logistikfunktion. Logistikfunktionens placering i organisationen behandles i afsnit 5.3.3.Large companies often set up a separate logistics department to ensure that the other departments "play together". Small and medium-sized companies may employ a single employee who is responsible for logistics. In small companies, it is not usually necessary to have a separate logistics function. The location of the logistics function in the organization is dealt with in section 5.3.3.

5.3.1 Arbejdsdeling5.3.1 Division of work

Som forklaret iafsnit 2.1er aktiviteterne i salgs-, indkøbs- og lagerafdelingen økonomiske processer, der udløser data (bilag), der registreres i virksomhedensERP-system. Dermed har virksomheden mulighed for at følge med i de økonomiske "spor", hver enkelt aktivitet fører med sig.As explained in section 2.1, the activities in the sales, purchasing and warehouse department are economic processes that trigger data (appendices) that are registered in the company's ERP system. This gives the company the opportunity to follow the financial "tracks" each individual activity leads with.

Myndighederne stiller også krav til virksomheden om, at der ved en handel skal foreligge skriftlig dokumentation i form af bilag. Det kan fx være fakturaer og kreditnotaer.The authorities also require the company to provide written documentation in the form of appendices for a transaction. This can be, for example, invoices and credit notes.

Når en virksomhed vokser i størrelse, bliver der behov for flere medarbejdere. Når der er mange ansatte i en virksomhed, skal arbejdsopgaverne fordeles mellem medarbejderne. Denne arbejdsdeling skal sikre, at varerne styres gennem virksomheden på den mest fornuftige, omkostningsbesparende og hurtigste måde.When a company grows in size, more employees are needed. When there are many employees in a company, the tasks must be distributed among the employees. This division of labor must ensure that the goods are managed through the company in the most reasonable, cost-saving and fastest way.

Ved arbejdsdelingen skal det fastlægges, hvordan arbejdsopgaverne skal fordeles mellem virksomhedens afdelinger.In the division of labor, it must be determined how the tasks are to be distributed between the company's departments.

Vi vil se på to forskellige principper for arbejdsdeling, nemlig:We will look at two different principles of division of labour, namely:

  • FunktionsprincippetThe operating principle
  • ObjektprincippetThe object principle

FunktionsprincippetThe operating principle

Funktionsprincippet er meget anvendt ved arbejdsdeling i danske virksomheder. En funktion er et antal arbejdsopgaver, der hører sammen, fx salg eller indkøb.The functional principle is widely used in the division of labor in Danish companies. A function is a number of work tasks that belong together, e.g. sales or purchasing.

Ved funktionsprincippet forstås en fordeling af arbejdsopgaver efter funktioner.The functional principle means a distribution of work tasks according to functions.

Hvis pengene er fremskaffet ved at hæve likvide midler i banken, medfører det et tab af renteindtægter.Inventory costs include those costs , associated with having an inventory. This primarily concerns interest on the capital tied up in the warehouse. After all, the goods on the company's inventory have been purchased and mostly paid for. The money to pay for the goods is obtained either by borrowing from, for example, the bank or by withdrawing liquid funds from the bank. If the money has been obtained through a loan, interest must be paid to the bank. If the money has been obtained by withdrawing liquid funds from the bank, this results in a loss of interest income.

Desuden er der omkostninger til selve driften af lageret, dvs. husleje, lys, varme og lønninger. Endelig kan der være tale om omkostninger i form af svind, dvs. varer som har mistet værdi pga. forældelse, eller fordi de er blevet beskadiget.In addition, there is costs for the actual operation of the warehouse, i.e. rent, electricity, heat and wages. Finally, it can be costs in the form of shrinkage, i.e. goods that have lost value due to obsolescence or because they have been damaged.

De nævnteomkostningerregistreres ikke som lageromkostninger i regnskabet. I den artsopdelteresultatopgørelseindgår de underandre eksterne omkostninger,personaleomkostninger, afskrivninger og renteomkostninger. Det kan derfor i praksis være vanskeligt at opgøre størrelsen af disse omkostninger.The mentioned costs are not recorded as inventory costs in the accounts. In the separate income statement, they are included under other external costs, personnel costs, depreciation and interest costs. It can therefore be difficult in practice to calculate the size of these costs.

Der vil være forskel på, hvor hårdt en virksomhed rammes aflageromkostninger. Eksempelvis vil lageromkostningerne for en virksomhed med positiv likvid beholdning være relativt lave. Pengene til køb af varelager er hævet på en indlånskonto med en lav rente. Rentetabet er derfor forholdsvist lille. Anderledes hårdt rammes en virksomhed, hvor pengene til varelageret er skaffet ved at hæve påkassekreditten, hvor renten er meget højere.There will be a difference in how hard a company is hit by storage costs. For example, the storage costs for a company with a positive cash balance will be relatively low. The money for the purchase of inventory has been withdrawn from a deposit account with a low interest rate. The interest loss is therefore relatively small. In a different way, a company where the money for the inventory has been obtained by drawing on the overdraft, where the interest rate is much higher, is hit hard.

Fashion Shoe har et lager af sko i butikken og et mindre lokale med ekstra størrelser. Skoene er betalt ved, at Rikke har hævet pengene på kassekreditten, hvor hun betaler 10 % i rente p.a. Herudover betaler Rikke selvfølgelig husleje, lys og varme for lagerlokalet. Der er ikke ansat egentligt lagerpersonale.Fashion Shoe has an inventory of shoes in the store and a smaller room with extra sizes. The shoes have been paid for by Rikke withdrawing the money from the overdraft, where she pays 10% interest p.a. In addition, Rikke of course pays rent, electricity and heat for the warehouse. There are no actual warehouse staff employed.

Disseomkostningervil normalt heller ikke umiddelbart være synlige i regnskabet, da de i den artsopdelte resultatopgørelse vil indgå under andre eksterne omkostninger, personaleomkostninger og afskrivninger.Packaging and packaging costs include costs for packaging materials, packaging machines and wages. These costs will not normally be immediately visible in the accounts either, since in the classified income statement they will be included under other external costs, personnel costs and depreciation.

TransportomkostningerTransport costs

Transportomkostninger består af både interne transportomkostninger mellem de enkelte afdelinger i virksomheden og eksterne transportomkostninger af varer fra leverandører og til kunder.Transport costs consist of both internal transport costs between the individual departments in the company and external transport costs of goods from suppliers and to customers.

Hvis virksomheden selv udfører transporten, eller hvis eksterne vognmænd foretager transporten for virksomhedens regning, vil det normalt være relativt let at opgøre disseomkostninger.If the company carries out the transport itself, or if external hauliers carry out the transport on behalf of the company, it will normally be relatively easy to calculate these costs .

Hvis omkostningerne for en varetransport fra en leverandør derimod er inkluderet i leverandørens pris for varerne, vil de i virksomhedensregnskaboptræde som en del af vareforbruget i den artsopdelte resultatopgørelse.If, on the other hand, the costs for a goods transport from an supplier are included in the supplier's price for the goods, they will appear in the company's accounts as part of the goods consumption in the type-divided income statement.

Administrative logistikomkostninger omfatter planlægnings- og kontrolomkostninger i forbindelse med kundeordrer, planlægning af indkøb og distribution.Administrative logistics costs include planning and control costs in connection with customer orders, planning purchases and distribution.

Det kan ofte være vanskeligt at opgøre disse omkostninger.A large part of the costs includes salaries and costs for the development and operation of IT systems. It can often be difficult to calculate these costs.

MangelomkostningerDeficiency costs

Mangelomkostninger er deomkostninger, der kan opstå, når virksomheden ikke overholder sine aftaler med kunderne. En sælger skal i henhold til købeloven levere varen i rette mængde, i rette kvalitet, i rette tid og på rette sted.Deficiency costs are the costs , which can arise when the company does not comply with its agreements with customers. According to the Sales Act, a seller must deliver the goods in the right quantity, in the right quality, at the right time and in the right place.

Nogle mangelomkostninger kan virksomheden beregne. Det gælder meromkostninger til fx flyfragt i stedet for skibsfragt, afhjælpning af fejl ude hos kunden og erstatning til kunden i forbindelse med manglende leveringsoverholdelse. Virksomheden kan også beregne, hvor meget den mister idækningsbidrag, hvis kunden annullerer en ordre på grund af fejl og mangler eller på grund af forsinkelser i leveringen.Some shortage costs can be calculated by the company. This applies to additional costs for e.g. air freight instead of ship freight, rectification of errors at the customer's disposal and compensation to the customer in connection with non-compliance with delivery. The company can also calculate how much it loses in contribution margin , if the customer cancels an order due to errors and omissions or due to delays in delivery.

Andre mangelomkostninger er det derimod ikke muligt at opgøre præcist. Det drejer sig om det dækningsbidrag, virksomheden kunne have indtjent på fremtidige ordrer fra kunder, der på grund af opståede fejl og mangler skifter til andre leverandører. I regnskabet kan man naturligvis ikke aflæse tab, der skyldes mistede ordrer i fremtiden. Sådanne tab kan imidlertid være af betydelig størrelse, da en mistet kunde fremover jo kunne have lagt mange store ordrer i virksomheden.Other shortage costs, on the other hand, are not possible to calculate precisely. It concerns the contribution margin, the company could have earned on future orders from customers who, due to errors and deficiencies, switch to other suppliers. In the accounts, you cannot of course read off losses due to lost orders in the future. However, such losses can be of considerable size, since a lost customer could have placed many large orders in the company in the future.

Fashion Shoe: MangelomkostningerFashion Shoe: Shortage Costs

Fashion Shoe har ikke mange mangelomkostninger. Hvis der er fejl på skoene, kan virksomheden blot sende disse tilbage til leverandøren.Fashion Shoe does not have many shortage costs. If the shoes are faulty, the company can simply send them back to the supplier.

Det kan selvfølgelig ske, at en kunde bliver utilfreds med ventetiden på sko, der skal repareres, ombyttes eller er i restordre. Konsekvensen kan være, at kunden holder op med at handle i butikken og måske omtaler virksomheden negativt, hvilket selvfølgelig kan betyde mindre fremtidigt salg.Of course, it can happen that a customer becomes dissatisfied with the waiting time for shoes that need to be repaired, exchanged or are on back order. The consequence may be that the customer stops shopping in the store and may speak negatively about the company, which of course can mean less future sales.

Det mistede dækningsbidrag vil være meget svært at opgøre.The lost contribution margin will be very difficult to account for.

5.4.2 Leveringsservice (C)5.4.2 Delivery service (C)

Leveringsserviceer rettet mod kunderne. Hvis virksomheden yder en god leveringsservice, kan dette være med til at skabe et større salg og dermed indtægter for virksomheden. Service er en vigtig konkurrenceparameter for virksomheder. Inden forlogistiklægges der især vægt på service under salget, og derfor bruges begrebet leveringsservice.Delivery service is aimed at customers. If the company provides a good delivery service, this can help create a bigger sale and thus revenue for the company. Service is an important competitive parameter for companies. Within logistics, particular emphasis is placed on service during the sale, and therefore the term delivery service is used.

Leveringsservicekan opdeles på følgende måde:Delivery service can be divided as follows:

  • LeveringstidDelivery time
  • LagerservicegradStock service grade
  • LeveringsoverholdelseDelivery compliance
  • LeveringsfleksibilitetDelivery flexibility
  • LeveringsinformationDelivery information

LeveringstidDelivery time

Leveringstiden er den tid, der går, fra virksomheden modtager ordren, til varerne ligger på det leveringssted, som er aftalt med kunden. Leveringstiden omfatter bl.a. den tid, der går med administrativ behandling af ordren. Hvis virksomheden ikke har de bestilte varer på lager, skal disse først bestilles hjem fra leverandøren. Leveringstiden fra leverandøren indgår derfor også i den samlede leveringstid. Endelig indeholder leveringstiden også den tid, der bruges til pakning og distribution af varerne.The delivery time is the time that elapses from the time the company receives the order until the goods are at the delivery location agreed with the customer. The delivery time includes i.a. the time spent on administrative processing of the order. If the company does not have the ordered goods on inventory, these must first be ordered home from the supplier. The delivery time from the supplier is therefore also included in the total delivery time. Finally, the delivery time also includes the time used for packing and distributing the goods.

Den øgede brug af IT i virksomhederne er med til at forkorte leveringstiden. IT kan anvendes til fx at udveksle informationer om lagerbeholdninger og tidspunkt for varens afsendelse. Selve bestillingen af varen foregår også ofte elektronisk.The increased use of IT in companies is helping to shorten the delivery time. IT can be used, for example, to exchange information about stock levels and the time of dispatch of the goods. The actual ordering of the item is also often done electronically.

Fashion Shoe: LeveringstidFashion Shoe: Delivery time

Fashion Shoe er en detailvirksomhed. Her kommer kunderne ind i butikken og køber sko. Normalt vil skoene være på lager, og de kan udleveres med det samme. Der vil derfor ikke være nogen leveringstid.Fashion Shoe is a retail business. Here, customers come into the store and buy shoes. Normally the shoes will be on inventory and they can be delivered immediately. There will therefore be no delivery time.

Det kan dog forekomme, at skoene er udsolgt og må bestilles hjem fra leverandøren fx Footwear. Leveringstiden vil så afhænge af, hvor lang leveringstiden er fra Footwear.However, it may happen that the shoes are sold out and must be ordered home from the supplier, e.g. Footwear. The delivery time will then depend on how long the delivery time is from Footwear.

LagerservicegradStock service grade

Lagerservicegraden er et udtryk for, hvor stor en andel af de indkomne ordrer der kan leveres direkte fra virksomhedens færdigvarelager. En lagerservicegrad på 80 % viser altså, at virksomheden er i stand til at ekspedere 80 % af alle modtagne ordrer direkte fra lageret.The warehouse service level is an expression of how large a proportion of the incoming orders can be delivered directly from the company's finished goods warehouse. A warehouse service level of 80% therefore shows that the company is able to dispatch 80% of all received orders directly from the warehouse.

De sidste 20 % af de modtagne ordrer er i såkaldt restordre.The last 20% of the orders received are in so-called backorder.

Fashion Shoe: LagerservicegradFashion Shoe: Warehouse Service Grade

I Fashion Shoe er lagerservicegraden ret høj. Normalt vil kunden købe et par sko, som er i butikken, og kunden kan derfor få varen med det samme.In Fashion Shoe, the warehouse service level is quite high. Normally, the customer wants to buy a pair of shoes that are in the store, and the customer can therefore get the product immediately.

Nogle gange kan den størrelse, som kunden ønsker, være udsolgt. Fashion Shoe bliver så nødt til at genbestille varen hos fx Footwear.Sometimes the size that the customer wants may be out of stock. Fashion Shoe will then have to re-order the item from e.g. Footwear.

LeveringsoverholdelseDelivery compliance

Leveringsoverholdelse kaldes også for leveringspræcision. Begge udtryk dækker over, hvor god virksomheden er til at overholde den indgåede aftale med kunden.Delivery compliance is also called delivery precision. Both expressions cover how good the company is at complying with the agreement entered into with the customer.

  • Er varerne leveret i rette tid?Are the goods delivered on time?
  • Er varerne leveret i rette mængde?Are the goods delivered in the right quantity?
  • Er varerne leveret på det aftalte sted?Are the goods delivered at the agreed place?
  • Er varerne leveret i rette kvalitet?Are the goods delivered in the right quality?

Forsinkelser fra sælgers side kan hos kunden føre til forsinkelser i dennes leverancer til sine kunder. Det kan i værste fald føre til krav om erstatning, ophævelse af indgåede kontrakter og dårligt omdømme. Overholdelse af leveringstiden er ofte vigtigere for kunden end en kort leveringstid. Virksomhedens kunder er normalt heller ikke interesserede i, at varerne kommer for tidligt. De har måske ikke ledig lagerplads, eller medarbejderne har måske ikke tid til at tage imod varerne.Delays on the part of the seller can lead to delays in the customer's deliveries to its customers. In the worst case, it can lead to claims for compensation, cancellation of concluded contracts and bad reputation. Compliance with the delivery time is often more important to the customer than a short delivery time. The company's customers are also usually not interested in the goods arriving too early. They may not have available storage space, or the employees may not have time to receive the goods.

Hvis kunden er en anden virksomhed, kan denne have flere afdelinger og dermed flere forskellige adresser. Det er derfor vigtigt, at leveringen sker på den rigtige adresse og ikke bare på en tilfældig af virksomhedens adresser.If the customer is another company, this may have several departments and thus several different addresses. It is therefore important that the delivery takes place to the correct address and not just to a random one of the company's addresses.

Det er også vigtigt, at varerne leveres til kunden i den aftalte mængde og i den aftalte kvalitet. Et svigt her kan medføre, at kunden ikke kan overholde indgåede aftaler med sine kunder.It is also important that the goods are delivered to the customer in the agreed quantity and in the agreed quality. A failure here may result in the customer not being able to comply with agreements entered into with its customers.

Fashion Shoe: LeveringsoverholdelseFashion Shoe: Delivery Compliance

I de fleste tilfælde vil Fashion Shoe have en rigtig god leveringsoverholdelse. Kunden får jo varen med hjem fra butikken, og den er derfor selvfølgelig leveret i rette tid, rette mængde, på rette sted og i rette kvalitet.In most cases, Fashion Shoe will have a very good delivery compliance. After all, the customer gets the item home from the store, and it is therefore of course delivered at the right time, in the right quantity, in the right place and in the right quality.

Der kan dog opstå problemer, hvis der er tale om en vare, som er udsolgt og derfor skal bestilles hos leverandøren. Her vil leveringsoverholdelsen afhænge af, om Fahion Shoe modtager skoene til tiden fra leverandøren.However, problems can arise if the item is sold out and therefore must be ordered from the supplier. Here, delivery compliance will depend on whether Fahion Shoe receives the shoes on time from the supplier.

LeveringsfleksibilitetDelivery flexibility

Leveringsfleksibilitet er et spørgsmål om virksomhedens evne til at klare uforudsete ændringer vedrørende leveringen. Der kan være, at kunden ønsker en større mængde end oprindeligt aftalt, hurtigere levering eller ændringer i de oprindeligt bestilte varianter og kvaliteter.Delivery flexibility is a question of the company's ability to cope with unforeseen changes regarding the delivery. It may be that the customer wants a larger quantity than originally agreed, faster delivery or changes to the variants and qualities originally ordered.

Inden for tøjbranchen sker det tit, at tøjbutikker med kort varsel ønsker yderligere leverancer af varer, hvis den aktuelle tøjmode viser sig at slå an. Imødekommelse af hasteordrer kan derfor være en vigtig konkurrenceparameter i denne branche.In the clothing industry, it often happens that clothing stores want additional deliveries of goods at short notice if the current clothing fashion proves to be popular. Accommodating rush orders can therefore be an important competitive parameter in this industry.

Fashion Shoe: LeveringsfleksibilitetFashion Shoe: Delivery flexibility

Leveringsfleksibilitet er normalt ikke nogen særlig vigtig faktor for Fashion Shoes kunder. For Fashion Shoe er det derimod meget vigtigt, at deres leverandør har en god leveringsfleksibilitet.Delivery flexibility is usually not a particularly important factor for Fashion Shoes customers. For Fashion Shoe, on the other hand, it is very important that their supplier has good delivery flexibility.

Det vil jo fx betyde, at Fashion Shoe hurtigt kan få leveret ekstra sko, hvis en model viser sig at være meget populær hos kunderne.This will mean, for example, that Fashion Shoe can quickly get extra shoes delivered if a model proves to be very popular with customers.

LeveringsinformationDelivery information

Leveringsinformation i rette tid kan være med til at give et godt samarbejde med kunden. En virksomhed bør informere sine kunder, så snart den opdager, at det ikke er muligt at overholde en indgået aftale. Kunderne skal jo også kunne give besked til deres kunder om forsinkelser eller restordrer. Ved større leverancer bør virksomheden underrette kunderne om, hvornår på dagen leverancen ankommer, så der er personale til stede til at modtage varerne.Delivery information at the right time can help to provide good cooperation with the customer. A company should inform its customers as soon as it discovers that it is not possible to comply with an agreement entered into. After all, customers must also be able to notify their customers about delays or back orders. In the case of larger deliveries, the company should inform the customers of what time of day the delivery will arrive, so that staff are present to receive the goods.

Mange kunder har i dag direkte elektronisk adgang til leverandørernes data om lagerbeholdninger og leveringstider. Kunderne kan også følge med i, hvor langt deres ordrer er kommet hos leverandørerne.Many customers today have direct electronic access to the suppliers' data on stocks and delivery times. Customers can also follow how far their orders have come with the suppliers.

Fashion Shoe: LeveringsinformationFashion Shoe: Delivery Information

Hos Fashion Shoe er det kun nødvendigt med leveringsinformation i de tilfælde, hvor virksomheden har bestilt nogle sko hjem til en kunde.At Fashion Shoe, delivery information is only necessary in cases where the company has ordered some shoes for a customer.

Fashion Shoe kan tilbyde kunden at ringe, når skoene er kommet hjem, eller hvis der sker forsinkelser.Fashion Shoe can offer the customer to call when the shoes have arrived or if there are delays.

LeveringsserviceDelivery service

En virksomheds leveringsservice opdeles i:A company's delivery service is divided into:

  • Leveringstid, dvs. tiden, fra virksomheden modtager en ordre, til varerne er leveret som aftaltDelivery time, i.e. the time from when the company receives an order until the goods are delivered as agreed

  • Indkøb efter bestillingspunkt– samme indkøbsmængdePurchase by order point – same purchase quantity
  • Periodisk indkøb– samme indkøbsintervallerPeriodic purchase – same purchase intervals

Indkøb efter bestillingspunktPurchase by point of order

Ved indkøb efterbestillingspunktfastsættes der en minimumsbeholdning for hver vare. Når beholdningen af varen når ned på det fastsatte minimum, bestiller virksomheden varen hos leverandøren. Ved denne metode er indkøbsmængden den samme fra gang til gang. Se eksempel iafsnit 5.7.When purchasing by order point, a minimum stock is set for each item. When the stock of the item reaches the set minimum, the company orders the item from the supplier. With this method, the purchase quantity is the same from time to time. See example in section 5.7.

Ved anvendelse af indkøb efter bestillingspunkt vil et uventet stort salg eller en større forsinkelse fra leverandørens side betyde, at virksomheden kommer til at mangle varer. Denne risiko kan dog begrænses ved, at virksomheden etablerer et sikkerhedslager, men dette vil jo forøge virksomhedenslageromkostninger. For at undgå dette kan virksomheden stille krav om kortere leveringstider og bedre leveringsoverholdelse fra leverandørerne.When using purchase by point of order, an unexpectedly large sale or a major delay on the part of the supplier will mean that the company will be short of goods. However, this risk can be limited by the company establishing a safety warehouse, but this will increase the company's storage costs. To avoid this, the company can demand shorter delivery times and better delivery compliance from suppliers.

Periodisk indkøbPeriodic purchase

Ved periodisk indkøb bestiller virksomheden varen på faste tidspunkter, fx hver dag, hver uge eller hver måned. Hvor tit, virksomheden vælger at indkøbe varen, afhænger bl.a. af varens holdbarhed. Ved denne metode er indkøbsmængden afhængig af, hvor meget der er solgt siden sidste bestilling. Virksomheden fylder nemlig ved hvert indkøb lageret op til en på forhånd fastsat maksimumbeholdning.With periodic purchases, the company orders the goods at fixed times, e.g. every day, every week or every month. How often the company chooses to purchase the item depends, among other things, on of the product's durability. With this method, the purchase quantity depends on how much has been sold since the last order. With each purchase, the company fills the warehouse up to a predetermined maximum stock.

Ved periodisk indkøb medfører et uventet stort salg eller en større forsinkelse fra leverandørens side, at virksomheden kommer til at mangle varer, hvis den ikke har et tilstrækkeligt stort sikkerhedslager. Der er vedperiodisk indkøbogså en risiko for, at der går for lang tid mellem de fastsatte tidspunkter for optælling, og at virksomheden dermed overser, at der pludselig mangler varer på lageret.In the case of periodic purchasing, an unexpectedly large sale or a major delay on the part of the supplier means that the company will be short of goods if it does not have a sufficiently large safety stock. With periodic purchases, there is also a risk that too much time elapses between the set times for counting, and that the company thus overlooks that there are suddenly no items in stock.

Ved valg af disponeringssystem må man prøve at veje fordele og ulemper ved de to systemer op mod hinanden. Ofte vil virksomheden anvende bestillingspunkt til de fleste varer, men anvende periodisk indkøb til varer med begrænset holdbarhed eller varer, hvor leveringssystemet betyder, at leverancer kun kan komme på ganske bestemte tidspunkter. Det vil i mange tilfælde være praktiske forhold der afgør, hvilken metode der vil være den bedste at anvende.When choosing a disposition system, one must try to weigh the advantages and disadvantages of the two systems against each other. Often the company will use point of order for most goods, but use periodic purchasing for goods with a limited shelf life or goods where the delivery system means that deliveries can only come at very specific times. In many cases, it will be practical conditions that determine which method will be the best to use.

I Fashion Shoe er der en række klassiske skomodeller, som går igen gennem flere sæsoner. De øvrige sko, som er mere sæsonbetonede indkøbes periodisk. Her indkøbes varerne i større mængder inden sæsonstart, idet det ikke altid kan lade sig gøre at genbestille modellerne, da disse kan være udsolgt fra fabrikken.The other shoes, which are more seasonal, are purchased periodically. LagermodelStock model

Når en virksomhed anvender indkøb efterbestillingspunkter indkøbsmængden den samme fra gang til gang. Som tidligere nævnt har indkøbsstørrelsen betydning for både virksomhedens lager- ogordreomkostninger. Virksomheden har mulighed for at beregneden optimale indkøbsstørrelse, dvs. den indkøbsmængde der giver de samlet set mindsteomkostninger.When a company uses purchasing by order point, the purchasing quantity is the same from time to time. As previously mentioned, the purchase size is important for both the company's inventory and order costs. The company has the opportunity to calculate the optimal purchase size, i.e. the purchase quantity that gives the overall smallest costs .

For at beregne den optimale indkøbsmængde, vil vi tage udgangspunkt i en forenklet lagermodel. Vi forudsætter, at der sker en jævn afgang fra lageret, så salget og dermed forbruget er det samme fra dag til dag. Det forudsættes også, at både leveringstiden og ordrestørrelsen er den samme fra gang til gang.In order to calculate the optimal purchase quantity, we will start from a simplified inventory model. We assume that there is an even departure from the warehouse, so that sales and thus consumption are the same from day to day. It is also assumed that both the delivery time and the order size are the same from time to time.

Disse forudsætninger betyder, at varelageret vil svinge mellem 0 stk. og et maksimumlager, der svarer til indkøbsstørrelsen.These prerequisites mean that the inventory will fluctuate between 0 units. and a maximum stock corresponding to the purchase size.

I eksemplet i figur 5.5 er det forudsat, at virksomheden indkøber 200 stk. pr. gang. Da salget forudsættes at foregår jævnt (dvs. det samme salg hver dag), vil størrelsen på lageret falde jævnt. Det forudsættes, at virksomheden modtager nye varer fra leverandøren lige præcis, når lageret er tomt. Når virksomheden modtager varer fra leverandøren, vil lageret derfor stige med den indkøbte mængde, her 200 stk.In the example in Figure 5.5, it is assumed that the company purchases 200 pcs. per walk. Since the sales are assumed to take place evenly (ie the same sales every day), the size of the inventory will decrease evenly. It is assumed that the company receives new goods from the supplier exactly when the warehouse is empty. When the company receives goods from the supplier, the stock will therefore increase with the purchased quantity, here 200 pcs.

Leverandøren har en vis leveringstid, derfor skal virksomheden bestille varerne, inden lageret er helt tomt. Hvis leveringstiden fra leverandøren fx er 14 dage, skal virksomheden altså bestille nye varer, når beholdningen på lageret svarer til 14 dages salg. Denne lagerstørrelse kaldes bestillingspunktet.The supplier has a certain delivery time, therefore the company must order the goods before the warehouse is completely empty. If the delivery time from the supplier is, for example, 14 days, the company must order new goods when the inventory in the warehouse corresponds to 14 days of sales. This stock size is called the order point.

Gennemsnitslageret i eksemplet i figur 5.5 kan beregnes som gennemsnittet af et minimumslager på 0 stk. og et maksimumlager på 200 stk. Det giver et gennemsnitslager på 100 stk., nemlig (0 stk. + 200 stk.):2.The average stock in the example in figure 5.5 can be calculated as the average of a minimum stock of 0 units. and a maximum stock of 200 pieces. Når en virksomhed foretager et indkøb, opstår derordreomkostningeroglageromkostninger.When a company makes a purchase, order costs and inventory costs occur.

Ordreomkostninger kaldes også bestillingsomkostninger eller indkøbsomkostninger. De omfatter især lønomkostninger for den tid, der går til behandling af den enkelte ordre. Det drejer sig om tid til bl.a. ordreafgivelse, varemodtagelse, kontrol,bogføringog betaling.Order costs are also called ordering costs or purchasing costs. In particular, they include labor costs for the time it takes to process the individual order. It concerns time for, among other things, order placement, goods receipt, control, bookkeeping and payment.

OrdreomkostningerOrder costs

Ordre- (eller indkøbsomkostninger) er deomkostninger, virksomheden må afholde i forbindelse med behandling af en ordre.Order (or purchase) costs are the costs the company must incur in connection with processing an order.

Lageromkostninger omfatter bl.a. renter af den investerede kapital i lageret, lønninger til lagerpersonale, omkostninger til lagerlokaler og svind på lageret.Storage costs include, among other things interest on the capital invested in the warehouse, wages for warehouse personnel, costs for warehouse premises and shrinkage in the warehouse.

LageromkostningerStorage costs

Lageromkostninger er de omkostninger, der knytter sig til opretholdelse af virksomhedens varelager. Dvs. renter af den kapital, der er bundet i lageret (pengebinding) samtomkostningertil selve driften af lageret, (husleje, lys, varme og lønninger).Inventory costs are those costs associated with maintaining the company's inventory. That is interest on the capital tied up in the warehouse (tied up money) as well as costs for the actual operation of the warehouse (rent, light, heat and wages).

Som nævnt handler indkøbsdisponering først og fremmest om at købe varer hjem i rette tid og i rette mængde. Store – og dermed få – indkøb vil føre til højelageromkostninger, mens ordreomkostningerne så til gengæld vil være lave. Små – og dermed mange – indkøb vil give høje ordreomkostninger, mens lageromkostningerne så til gengæld vil være lave.As mentioned, purchasing planning is primarily about buying goods home at the right time and in the right quantity. Large – and thus few – purchases will lead to high inventory costs, while order costs will then be low. Small – and thus many – purchases will result in high order costs, while the storage costs will then be low.

Optimal indkøbsstørrelseOptimal purchase size

Den optimale indkøbsstørrelsepr. gang er den mængde, hvor summen af de årlige lageromkostninger ogordreomkostningerer lavest.The optimal purchase size per times is the quantity where the sum of the annual inventory costs and order costs is the lowest.

Varelagre i produktionsvirksomhedenInventory in the production company

Enproduktionsvirksomhedadskiller sig fra en handels- ellerservicevirksomhedved, at den selv producerer de varer, den sælger videre.A manufacturing company differs from a trading or service company in that it itself produces the goods it resells.

Produktionsvirksomheden indkøber råvarer, halvfabrikata og forskellige komponenter fra leverandører, der også kaldes råvareleverandører eller underleverandører. Produktionsvirksomheden forarbejder herefter de indkøbte varer til færdigvarer, som så sælges videre.The production company purchases raw materials, semi-finished products and various components from suppliers, which are also called raw material suppliers or sub-suppliers. The production company then processes the purchased goods into finished goods, which are then sold on.

Som det fremgår af figuren, har en produktionsvirksomhed tre former for varelagre:As can be seen from the figure, a manufacturing company has three types of inventories:

  • RåvarelagerRaw material warehouse
  • Lager af varer under fremstilling i produktionsafdelingeninventory of goods under manufacture in the production department
  • Færdigvarelager.Finished goods warehouse.

For at virksomheden kan producere varer, skal den bruge forskellige input i form afproduktionsmidler:In order for the company to produce goods, it must use various inputs in the form of means of production:

  • RåvarerRaw materials
  • Arbejdskraft ogLabor and
  • MaskinerMachines

Logistikprocessen i en produktionsvirksomhedThe logistics process in a manufacturing company

Iafsnit 5.2gennemgik vi logistikprocessen i enhandelsvirksomhed. I en produktionsvirksomheden udvides logistikprocessen med en produktionsafdeling samt de to varelagre råvarelager og varer under fremstilling. Som vist i følgende figur udvides antal aktiviteter dermed også:In section 5.2, we reviewed the logistics process in a trading company. In a production company, the logistics process is expanded with a production department as well as the two warehouses for raw materials and goods under manufacture. As shown in the following figure, the number of activities is thus also expanded:

Vi har øverst i figuren (fra venstre mod højre) virksomhedens afdelinger for salg, indkøb og produktion. Det er de afdelinger, der planlægger, hvordan de modtagne ordrer skal effektueres.At the top of the figure (from left to right) we have the company's departments for sales, purchasing and production. It is the departments that plan how the received orders are to be implemented.

Nederst i figuren vises (fra højre mod venstre) varestrømmene. Varerne leveres fra virksomhedens leverandører og lægges på råvarelageret. Når råvarerne senere er forarbejdet og solgt, leveres de til kunden. Varestrømmen viser samtidigden eksterne transportaf varerne fra leverandører til produktionsvirksomheden og videre til kunderne.At the bottom of the figure, the product flows are shown (from right to left). The goods are delivered from the company's suppliers and placed in the raw material warehouse. When the raw materials are later processed and sold, they are delivered to the customer. The goods flow also shows the external transport of the goods from suppliers to the production company and on to the customers.

Den eksterne transportforegår med enten eksterne distributører (vognmænd og speditører) eller med produktionsvirksomhedens egne chauffører og lastbiler.The external transport takes place with either external distributors (truckers and freight forwarders) or with the production company's own drivers and trucks.

Se ses, at den interne transport af varer omfatter transport fra råvarelageret til produktionsafdelingen og videre til færdigvarelageret.It can be seen that the internal transport of goods includes transport from the raw materials warehouse to the production department and on to the finished goods warehouse.

Endelig indeholder figuren en angivelse af de ordrer, der går mellem virksomhedens interne afdelinger og mellem virksomheden og dens eksterne interessenter, dvs. kunder, leverandører og distributører.Finally, the figure contains an indication of the orders that go between the company's internal departments and between the company and its external stakeholders, i.e. customers, suppliers and distributors.

Det viste eksempel begynder med, at virksomhedens salgsafdeling modtager en såkaldt ekstern ordre (salgsordre) fra en kunde (1). Salgsafdelingen undersøger straks, om varerne kan leveres direkte fra færdigvarelageret (2). Hvis dette ikke er muligt, går der en intern ordre (rekvisition) til virksomhedens produktionsafdeling (3), som straks gennem en intern ordre (produktionsordre) sørger for, at produktionen af de bestilte varer går i gang (4).The example shown begins with the company's sales department receiving a so-called external order (sales order) from a customer (1). The sales department immediately investigates whether the goods can be delivered directly from the finished goods warehouse (2). If this is not possible, an internal order (requisition) goes to the company's production department (3), which immediately, through an internal order (production order), ensures that the production of the ordered goods starts (4).

Produktionsafdelingen beder samtidig indkøbsafdelingen undersøge, om virksomheden har de råvarer på lager, der skal bruges til den pågældende produktion (5). Hvis dette ikke er tilfældet, afgiver indkøbsafdelingen en ekstern ordre (indkøbsordre) til virksomhedens leverandører (6).At the same time, the production department asks the purchasing department to investigate whether the company has the raw materials on inventory that must be used for the production in question (5). If this is not the case, the purchasing department issues an external order (purchase order) to the company's suppliers (6).

Når de bestilte råvarer modtages fra leverandørerne, indgår de på virksomhedens råvarelager (7), hvorefter de overføres til produktionen for at indgå i produktionsprocessen (8). Når varerne er færdigproducerede, overføres de til virksomhedens færdigvarelager (9), hvorefter de distribueres til kunden (10).When the ordered raw materials are received from the suppliers, they are included in the company's raw material warehouse (7), after which they are transferred to production to be included in the production process (8). When the goods are finished, they are transferred to the company's finished goods warehouse (9), after which they are distributed to the customer (10).

Det bemærkes at økonomiafdelingen ikke er del af selve logistikprocessen. Årsagen er, at økonomiafdelingen ikke direkte bidrager til sikre planlægning, gennemførelse og kontrol af en effektivvarestrømfra leverandørerne gennem virksomheden og ud til kunderne.It is noted that the finance department is not part of the logistics process itself. The reason is that the finance department does not directly contribute to safe planning, implementation and control of an efficient flow of goods from the suppliers through the company and out to the customers.

LogistikprocessenThe logistics process

Logistikprocessen er summen af de aktiviteter, der sættes i gang, fra virksomheden modtager en ordre, til den er leveret. Økonomiafdelingens aktiviteter er ikke en del af logistikprocessen.The logistics process is the sum of the activities that are set in motion from the time the company receives an order until it is delivered. The activities of the finance department are not part of the logistics process.
