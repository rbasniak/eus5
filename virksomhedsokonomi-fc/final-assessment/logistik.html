Logistics

English narration with Danish pronunciation for technical terms.

Slide 1

Today I will focus on two logistics questions: the bestillingspunkt and the use of a sikkerhedslager.

Shaping New Tomorrow sells about 10,000 pairs per year, the leveringstid is 14 days, and the optimal indkøbsstørrelse is 1,250 units.

Slide 2

Before we calculate the bestillingspunkt, this is the cost context.

The optimal indkøbsstørrelse is 1,250 units, so Shaping New Tomorrow buys 8 times per year.

At this point, lageromkostninger and ordreomkostninger are almost equal, and the total annual cost is the lowest.

Slide 3

Here we calculate the bestillingspunkt.

Shaping New Tomorrow expects to sell 10,000 units per year, which is about 27 units per day.

With 14 days of leveringstid, the expected demand is around 389.

Slide 4

The bestillingspunkt tells Shaping New Tomorrow when to order again.

When the stock reaches about 389 units, Shaping New Tomorrow places a new order.

The remaining stock should cover normal sales during the 14-day leveringstid, so the company does not run out before the delivery arrives.

The 389-unit bestillingspunkt assumes normal sales and exactly 14 days of leveringstid.

In practice, delivery can be late, sales can be higher, or the supplier can have problems.

Slide 5

Sikkerhedslager gives extra protection.

For example, 430 units could be used, but it is only an example, not a calculated target.

Sikkerhedslager has clear benefits: it lowers the risk of stock-outs and improves customer service.

Slide 6

But there is a cost.

More stock means more capitalbinding and higher lageromkostninger.

With a kostpris of 347.70 kroner per unit and a lagerrente of 8.66 percent, extra stock should only be kept when the risk justifies it.

Slide 7

My recommendation is simple.

First, keep the optimal indkøbsstørrelse at 1,250 units.

Second, use 389 units as the normal bestillingspunkt.

Third, add a sikkerhedslager only when the actual delivery and demand risk requires it.

The goal is good availability without unnecessary capitalbinding.
