Shaping New Tomorrow

English narration with Danish pronunciation for technical terms.

Choose a slide to start its audio.

Slide 1

As an introduction, Shaping New Tomorrow is a handelsvirksomhed operating within detailhandel, with clothing as its principal product category.

Its ejerform is an anpartsselskab (ApS), the anpartskapitalen is approximately 63.000kr.

Today, the company is owned by Shaping New Tomorrow Holding ApS.

Today I'll explain Shaping New Tomorrow's indtjeningsevne and likviditet from 2018/19 to 2020/21.

The company grows from 2 to 6 stores, and nettoomsætning rises strongly.

The key question is whether this growth also improves profitability.

Slide 2

First, we see very strong growth in nettoomsætning.

It increases from 78.6 to 300 million kroner, and the indeks goes from 100 to 382.

This is positive, but growth alone doesn't show if Shaping New Tomorrow earns efficiently.

Slide 3

Here I look at BAP (Bruttoavanceprocent) and DG (Dækningsgrad).

Both are fairly stable over the three years, and in year 3 they are close to or above year 1.

This means the main problem is not vareforbrug or variable omkostninger, but costs later in the resultatopgørelse.

Slide 4

The main issue is kapacitetsomkostninger.

Their index reaches 886, compared with 382 for nettoomsætning.

A likely reason is the expansion from 2 to 6 stores, which increases rent, staff and other fixed operating costs.

Slide 5

This slide shows the effect on indtjeningsevne.

Årets resultat før renter rises, but OG (Overskudsgrad) falls from 37% to 16%.

So Shaping New Tomorrow earns more in total, but much less for each krone of sales.

Slide 6

To improve resultatet, Shaping New Tomorrow should focus on profitability per store.

Management should control kapacitetsomkostninger, measure the return from e-shop and promotion, and only open new stores when break-even and cash needs are clear.

The high index for eksterne omkostninger and afskrivninger shows why this is important.

Slide 7

Finally, resultat and likviditet are not the same.

Cash can be tied up in lager and investments even when the company has a profit.

Shaping New Tomorrow should reduce lagerbinding, negotiate longer payment terms, and phase investments more carefully.

The goal is profitable growth with stronger cash flow.
